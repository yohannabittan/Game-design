## What went stale or got in the way

- **The docs check:** only `skeleton/README.md` is old (40 days).
- **Builders hunted for:** `exact.mjs`'s `boot` had no `dpr`, so both body builders copied it to make 4x sheets (fixed below); where every unlock is drawn (`unlockMark`); that the engine reads only `docs/art/candidates/arena/candidates.json` (the orchestrator had put Packs 33 and 34 in their own folders); that `SHOULDER` is fight geometry; where each figure draws; render caches leaking into replay dumps. All but the first are now in `RECIPES.md`.
- **What the designer had to say twice:** none twice; but four look faults reached the designer that a 4x still against a known-good figure would have shown (the arm off the body, the frontal chest, the shoulder, the kilt).
- **Cards and briefs out of date:** the Artist brief's "PIL is broken" (wrong both ways: the default `python3`'s PIL is broken, 3.13's works), reported by three art sessions; the Sketcher card's kit is landscape, reported by both Ronin sketchers.
- **A tool that lost work silently:** `land.mjs` bumped `CACHE_VERSION` in a copy of `sw.js` read before the merge, dropping every builder's list edits from v87 to v94; Arena had not loaded offline since the knife trials. Found while landing v94; fixed in v95 with a smoke check that every `src` and `assets` file is listed.
- **Parallel builds:** Arena builders ran alongside Launch and art sessions and landed cleanly through the three-way merge; two Arena builders on different features were rare (the shared rig kept the body builds in sequence). The one landing fault was the tool above, not a conflict.
- **The orchestrator missed finished art sessions:** two art sessions sat finished for hours until the designer asked "anything running?"; remote sessions don't notify.
