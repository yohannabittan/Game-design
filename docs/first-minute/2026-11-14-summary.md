# The five mini games' first minute, on one page (2026-11-14)

Two helper agents played each game's friends' release copy (v1.6) on a fresh save, tapping like someone who had read nothing. The full looks, with frames and GIFs, are in `2026-11-14-golf-ink-recoil.md` and `2026-11-14-launch-checkpoint.md`. An agent can say what the screen does, not whether it's fun.

## What all five share

- **None says why a try failed.** A miss in Recoil, a strike in Checkpoint, a time-out in Ink and a wasted shot in Golf all end without a reason. Principle 18 asks for a word.
- **The main skill is told, or left to be found, never done.** Recoil's kick, Launch's boost, Ink's needle and Checkpoint's "what counts" are each the whole game, and none is taught by doing.
- **A story stands between the newcomer and the first touch** in every game (2 to 4 taps, 4 to 6 seconds), and each story card swallows the first tap.

## Game by game, in the order a small fix would buy the most

| # | Game | What a newcomer meets | The single best fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Recoil** | The first shot scores with no drag, so the gun is never moved. Fast taps hit 2 of 8 and a miss shows only the barrel kick. Left-side taps do nothing, silently. But its result card is the best of the five: "150, New best! 690 to ★". | **A one-word reason on a miss** ("HIGH", "LOW", "EARLY"), and the first target off the gun's line so the drag is used. |
| 2 | **Ink** | Quickest to play (4 seconds). The first scribble is ruined in about 3 seconds: nothing shows that the needle leads the finger, or what a slip is. A time-out at 68% shows the same sad client and line as a ruined piece. | **Show the needle before the first touch, with a ghost drag,** and say why a try ended ("Time ran out at 68%. 85% for a star."). |
| 3 | **Launch** | A flight with no input at all lasts 18 seconds, says "New best!" and ticks off a goal. The boost is never needed, so it may never be found. | **Teach the boost by doing, in the first flight,** with a ghost thumb. |
| 4 | **Checkpoint** | Tapping what looks suspicious loses in about 14 seconds to three strikes with no explanation. (Body scans are already limited to travellers who beep.) | **Day 1 forgives the first false alarms and names the item** that should have been caught. |
| 5 | **Gravity Golf** | The healthiest: the aim preview teaches the shot, and there's no way to lose. Four taps and 6 seconds of story come before the first drag, and the star target is the smallest text on the result card. | **Play the story after hole 1,** and make "3 stars: 1 shot" the headline of the card. |

**Not verified:** in Gravity Golf, one scripted upward drag sent the ball down while the preview pointed up. It may have been the script; worth one feel.

## What it would cost

Each best fix is one small build on one game, and different games can build side by side. All five: about US$30 to 40 and half a day of agents, each with a first-minute GIF before and after. Then a v1.7 for friends.
