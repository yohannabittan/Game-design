## Launch

**Files:** `F`launch-first-minute-idle.gif (touch only the machine, then wait), `F`launch-first-minute-verbs.gif (skip, launch, then boost, glide, slam). Frames in `F`launch/: story-1, menu, aim-hint, gauge-hint, flight-hint, verbs-grid, over-idle, over-active.

**Opening to real play.** A four-card story plays itself (about 16 s untouched, 7 s if you tap through). Skip is on screen. Menu, then Play. Fastest: 2 taps (Skip, Play), about 2 s. Tapping the story through: 5 taps, about 8 s. The first real input (lock the barrel) is tap 3 or 6.

**Verbs:**
| Verb | How it is met |
| --- | --- |
| Tap to lock the barrel | Told: "Tap to lock the barrel", pulsing, first two flights |
| Tap in the gold to fire | Told: "Tap when the needle is in the gold" (7 words). The gold is a thin arc on a dial about 25 px wide |
| Hold to boost | Told once, in a pill at the top of the first arc, not done |
| Swipe up to glide | Same pill |
| Swipe down to slam | Same pill. A slam on nothing gives the word "Missed" and no reason |
| Tap for a short burst | Never told. Found by accident |
A newcomer who does nothing in flight loses nothing. My idle flight ran 18 s, went 279 m, ticked the "Bounce off a bird" goal with no input, and said "New best!". Glide, slam and the burst are likely never used.

**Text.** Story: 50 words in 4 cards, longest 14 ("Every morning in the bakery window..." and the Mochi Maker card). Hints: 5, 7 and 8 words. Menu: three goal rows. Result card: about 40 words, with "Weak launch, combo x2" as jargon.

**Answers.** A launch gets a zone word (Perfect, Great, Good, Weak), steam, pistons and a flash; Perfect also gets slow-motion. Good and bad launches are different on screen, but the dial is tiny and the whole flight is the same length. Flights have no failing, only caramel ("Stuck!", taught once). Good and bad flights both end "New best!" plus sugar. A bad launch never says why. The only reason given is the word "Weak" on the result card.

**After the end.** Launch Again is 1 tap, after about 1.4 s of card slide. Reasons to go again: a goals list (each with sugar), "New best!", Mochi's line, a new-place ticket (my second flight: 519 m, "Candy Meadow +25 sugar"). What sugar buys is not said on the card.

**Broken or confusing:**
- Menu shows an EXPORT button, four empty dotted circles, an unlabelled journey strip and Story. None is explained.
- Two round dials on screen (machine dial and the top speedometer). The hint says "needle" (not verified whether newcomers mix them up).
- Fizz pips drain on a boost with no label.
- No errors in the console.

**Three cheapest changes:**
1. **Best: teach boost by doing in flight one.** At the arc top, a ghost thumb holds for boost, then fades; glide and slam wait for flights 2 and 3, one each. Evidence: idle flights are 18 s, pay out and tick a goal, so nothing pushes a newcomer to touch the screen; the three-verb pill is 8 words read mid-flight (principle 18).
2. **Say why a launch was weak.** Result card shows the zone, a mark where the needle stopped against the gold, and "Perfect adds about X m" using real numbers. Evidence: the only cause given is "Weak launch"; the dial gold is 25 px (principles 19 and 21).
3. **Open on the machine, story after the first flight.** Evidence: 16 s of auto-play card before the first thing a player can touch, and the menu is a wall of things in between (principle 21).

**Cannot judge:** whether the launch feels good, the sound, whether a person mixes the two dials, how long the 18 s flight feels to a real player.

## Checkpoint

**Files:** `F`checkpoint-first-minute-active.gif (taps on blue items), `F`checkpoint-first-minute-idle.gif (no taps in play). Frames in `F`checkpoint/: story-1, menu, brief, idle-play-frames, active-play-frames, body-scan-frame, over-card.

**Opening to real play.** Three story cards play themselves (about 11 s; Skip exists in code). Menu with ten days, nine locked. Day 1, then a brief card with Start. Fastest: 2 taps. With three story taps I was playing at 7.7 s. Retry as the 2nd attempt: 1 tap (not verified that it skips the brief).

**Verbs:**
| Verb | How it is met |
| --- | --- |
| Tap a contraband item on a belt bag | Shown: brief card pictures knife, scissors, gun and leg knife. "Tap" is never said |
| Tap the item on the body scan after a beep | Told: "Beep means look" (does not say tap) |
| Tap a traveller who carries something | Left to be found |
| Leave clean items and people alone | Day 1: not told (the green-light tip is on a later card); learned by a strike |
| Rush lever (belt faster) | Left to be found; no label on day 1 |

**Text.** Cold open: 10, 10 and 11 words. Brief: sub-line 9 words, Pat's line 7, four item labels. In play: only bubbles ("Is this about my shoes?"), "+n, early", "False alarm", "Missed: scissors". Nothing says what a strike is.

**Answers.** A catch: gold burst, floating points, "SEARCH" stamp, rising tone. A false alarm: red flash, shake, "False alarm", and a strike X. A miss: "Missed: item" and a strike X. With the sound off the catch and the false alarm look different (gold against red, plus the X). A false alarm does not say what the item was or that it was harmless. The end card is a newspaper ("SHIFT CUT SHORT AT GATE 4", "The flight didn't take off. Passengers are waiting while it's searched") with "Missed 0, false alarms 3".

**After the end.** Retry is 1 tap (after the front page settles, about 1.5 s). Reasons: "3 stars: no strikes. 2: one. 1: clear.", the score, and Pat's "We'll get them off tomorrow."

**The overload the designer felt.** It is already narrower than the brief says: the detector gate shipped, so only travellers who beep get a body scan, and it shows for 1.4 s. What is left to read at once in the first 20 s: two bags of 5 to 8 items, a queue of three people, an arch, speech bubbles that sit on top of the scan (frame body-scan-frame), strike pips, streak and a lever. The lane is a column about 150 px wide against 235 px of belt. How often travellers beep on day 1: not measured.

**Broken or confusing:** a false alarm costs the same as a miss. Tapping blue things (my bot, "metal is blue") gave 3 false alarms and the shift ended about 14 s into play. Idle: first strike about 5 s in, shift over by about 20 s. EXPORT button shows on the menu. No console errors.

**Three cheapest changes:**
1. **Best: day 1 forgives false alarms and names them.** First two false alarms cost no strike, and the label reads "Phone: fine" instead of "False alarm". Evidence: my run lost in 14 s to 3 unexplained strikes, and the only list of what is bad appears once, before play (principle 18's "a word for why a try didn't count").
2. **Day 1 is bags only; the arch and the scan arrive on day 2.** Or on day 1 the first beeper pauses for a ghost thumb on the item. Evidence: the arch, scan and bubbles share the screen with the belt in the first 20 s; this is the "too much" note. Check first that `TUNING` lets day 1 turn beeps off (not verified).
3. **Keep the four contraband pictures on screen** as a small strip on day 1. Evidence: they appear once on the brief card, then 5 to 8 items per bag go by and a newcomer is guessing (principle 18).

**Cannot judge:** whether the scan is "too much" for a person (I only count what is on screen), the sound, how often travellers beep, whether Retry skips the brief.
