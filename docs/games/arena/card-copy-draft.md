# Arena card copy, draft 1 (2026-10-15)

The designer said: "The cards can be confusing." This draft rewrites every card in plain words.

## The card face

1. **The effect's name,** large: for example "Plate Breaker".
2. **One line in plain words,** saying what you will notice in a fight.
3. **A small footer:** the gear piece, its slot and its family, for example "Horned Cap · helmet · Brute".

## Words the cards stop using

| Old word | Plain word |
| --- | --- |
| slot, slot hit | gap: the glowing openings in his armour; a stab into a gap |
| tell | his wind-up |
| pips | Momentum (the diamonds under your hearts) |
| perfect block | a block right as his blow lands |
| winded | out of breath, open to free hits |
| lame | cripple: that leg or arm goes slow |
| bleed, stack, tick | bleeding: he loses a little health every second |
| overhead | a smash from above |
| jab | a quick stab |
| feint read | spotting his fake |
