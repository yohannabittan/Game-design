# Concept: Arena

- **Slug:** arena
- **Pattern:** a two-thumb duel (aim the sword's tip, read the tells), with L8, the wheel of fate, for the fighter and the upgrade draft
- **Date:** 2026-10-08, written after 13 feel prototypes (`prd-v0.1.md`, protos 1 to 13)
- **Status:** concept, drafted for graduation from prototype mode. The designer finalises it after the proto 13 playtest.

## One sentence

Roll a gladiator on the wheels of fate, then fight a gauntlet of five rolled opponents with a sword you aim like a trackpad: read their tells, slip your blade into the gaps in their armour, and pick upgrades between bouts.

## The first ten seconds

Five wheels spin out your fighter: size, three stats, armour, then your weapon. A card names you: "Lucia, a baker from Capua. Fights for: Debt." Old Brutus grunts a line. You tap Fight! and face a sparring partner across the sand. His sword glows high, so your left thumb taps HIGH and the blade rings off your shield. Your right thumb flicks forward and the sword's tip goes into the gap at his armpit.

## The fantasy

You are a nobody fighting your way out of the pit. Mastery is reading a feint without flinching, and answering with one clean thrust into the slit between two plates.

## Context, story, characters, emotion

- **Context:** a training pit and then the arena, in an invented Roman world.
- **Story:** each run is one fighter's story. They fight for freedom, a debt, revenge or glory; they meet a rival champion who remembers them; they end on an epilogue, or with their name chalked on the pit wall.
- **Characters:** your rolled fighter, Old Brutus the lanista (your trainer), and the champion who becomes your rival.
- **Emotion:** the underdog's grit. The payoff is beating the rival who beat you last time.

## Three tips

1. Wait for the glow to settle: a feint shimmers violet, so switch your shield to where it lands.
2. Thrust, don't slash, at the dark slits. A jab along the gap does three times the damage.
3. Make him spend his stamina, then step in on OPEN! and stab the glowing gaps.

## Perfect versus sloppy

| | Perfect | Sloppy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The defence | You read the feint and switch the block | You flinch at the fake and eat the real strike |
| The attack | You step in when he is winded and jab into his open slots | You rake slashes across his plates |
| What the game does | "Feint read!", an S grade and an epic card in the draft | A C grade, half a heart lost, and common cards |

## What it borrows

From Swords and Sandals, the gladiator fantasy, the rolled fighter and the gold between bouts. From wheel-of-fate videos, the visible odds and the tiers. From the samurai platformer idea, the precise cut.

## Guessed kernel

Proto 13 as it stands, rebuilt cleanly:
- **Verb:** aim the sword's tip with the right thumb, and block or step with the left.
- **Skill axis:** reading tells and finding the gaps.
- **Content:** a five-opponent gauntlet built from rolled fighters, about 26 upgrade cards, and four weapons.
- **What ends a session:** the champion falls, or you do.
