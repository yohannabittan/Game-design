1. **The City is beatable now,** with the pair heal and whole hearts.
2. **The first finishers fired:** Execute and two Second Breaths in one run.
3. **The ludus and the spoils are used:** the trident, and a weapon taken.
4. **The Colosseum's first beast is a wall.** Three of five runs ended at the bout 2 beast. A beast's size is fixed: the lion is always big and the bear always a giant, whatever the bout. So bout 2 meets a bout 4-sized animal.
5. **The drive is barely used:** 0 to 3 drives a run, about 0.07 H in all. Nothing on screen says it exists.
6. **The crowd works:** up to 68 in a good run, and low in short lost ones.
