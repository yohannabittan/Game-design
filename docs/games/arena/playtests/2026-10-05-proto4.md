# Playtest: Arena proto 4 (two thumbs, straight arm, shield)

- **Date:** 2026-10-05, from the designer's export (9 rounds) and notes.
- **Viewport:** the export says **402x812, portrait**, but proto 3's was 874x402. If the phone's rotation lock was on, the landscape game was squeezed into portrait, which alone makes aiming hard.

## Findings

- **"I struggled this time."** No opponent was felled in 9 rounds.
- **The body leans forward too easily**, which makes the sword hard to aim. "The sword should lead, and the character should only lean if I'm slashing out for extra reach or power." The legs were hard to reach without pulling back, and gaps could barely be targeted. The data agrees: up to 28 jabs gave 4 gap hits.
- **Every counter landed** (counterHits = counters): counters are too fast to answer.
- **The shield is "a bit better"**, with 2 perfect blocks in one round. "We need a button for shield up, mid or down."

## Proto 5 direction

1. **Stance:**
   - an upright body;
   - the sword leads;
   - a lean only on a fast slash past the arm's reach;
   - the hand can drop low to reach the legs.
2. **Shield:** three buttons on the left edge, high, middle and low, matching the enemy's tells; a swipe back stays the dodge.
3. **Softer counters.**
4. **A fairer jab hit radius.**
5. **A "turn your phone sideways" card in portrait.**
