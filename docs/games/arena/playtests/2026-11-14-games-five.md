## Read

- **Overheads are being thrown:** 14 in five runs, against almost none in the dozen runs before. This is still v69, before Step 26b; the planted overhead (Step 26) seems to have helped by itself.
- **The Games is a wall at the start.** Five runs, none past bout 3, two ended by a spearman (Castor both times). Earlier Games runs include three wins, so it can be beaten; these fighters were B and A tier with one or two plates.
- **Spearmen again:** runs 1 and 5 were lost to Castor's spear, with 40 lunges in run 5. The spear step (`../steps/2026-11-14-spear.md`) only lengthens your own spear; it does not change how hard it is to get inside his with a short weapon. Worth watching.
- **Two swords was tried once more** (run 3), after the two-spear run. Lost in bout 2. The two-weapon sketches are with the designer.
- **Bleed keeps carrying the longest runs** (run 4 here; the Rome run earlier today).
