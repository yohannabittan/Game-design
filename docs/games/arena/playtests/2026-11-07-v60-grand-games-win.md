# Playtest 2026-11-07: arena-v60 (dev, 874x402), a Grand Games win with a bleed build

**The designer:** "A character retooled from A to S with a bleed build. I should have bought more healing at the end, before the champion. I couldn't really tell the champions' different specials apart, but overall it was cinematic, the way he switched to two blades, and my character's stuff all felt good and fun."

**The export's runs since v57:**

| Arena | Bought | Reached | Grades | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grand Games | two sicas, no armour | bout 3 | A C | lost to Mevia (giant, spear) |
| City | axe, helmet + chest | bout 4 | S S C | lost to Pugnax (giant, axe) |
| Grand Games | gladius, helmet + chest; Strength respun to S, giant | won, 4 bouts | B A C A | **won**, thumbs up, favour 69 |

**The win:**
- the bleed build was Serrated, Bloodlust, Rend, Cruel and Vein, with 107.5 bleed damage (the most in any export);
- 18 hits taken against a giant's double hearts;
- only one signature move logged (`capuan1`, Varro's), and none of the others shows in `moves`.

**Read:**
- the specials fire rarely and aren't told apart;
- the two-blade switch landed;
- the bandage choice before the champion matters, and the designer learned it the hard way.
