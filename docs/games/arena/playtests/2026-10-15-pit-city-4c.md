**Findings:**
1. **Losses now pay fame,** as step 4c intends: 9 for a loss at bout 4, and 7 for a loss at bout 3 with 69 gold left.
2. **Still no finisher has fired.** Momentum gained fell to 2 to 10 a run, against 7 to 15 earlier. Only one run held war paint (Execute, 3 pips), and it gained 3 Momentum in the whole run.
3. **The ludus is still unused.** No trident or sica and no new origin turned up in nine runs.
4. **Giants come up often.** There were 11 giants in 9 runs, where the gauntlet weights give about 4 or 5. Three of the losses were to a giant. One cause: the champion who fells you comes back as your rival, so a giant champion repeats. It is worth a quick check that nothing else skews the roll.
5. **The crowd stays low:** 7 to 52 at a bout's end. The two 4c runs are too few to judge the slower drain.
6. **The run went 1 for 9.** Three runs ended at bout 1 or 2, some of them with experimental picks (Showboat and Flourish; your own feint).
