# Playtest: Arena proto 13, the first win

- **Date:** 2026-10-08. Read from the designer's export and their note: "Wooooooo I managed to win."
- **Build:** arena-v15, dev channel, played in landscape (874x402).

## The winning run

- **The fighter:** a fighter from Alexandria, M, STR 3, SPD 4, STA 4, medium armour (head, chest and legs plated), a spear, tier A. Reason: Glory.
- **The gauntlet:**
  - S dagger;
  - M axe;
  - L gladius;
  - S spear;
  - the champion, Marcus the Red, L gladius. He had beaten this designer in 4 earlier runs.
  
  **Rival defeated.**
- **The numbers:**
  - 13 slot hits and 4 punishes;
  - 25 blocks, 13 of them perfect;
  - 11 hits taken, against 6 half-hearts;
  - **the first feint ever read**: a switch read;
  - grades B, C, B, B, B;
  - picks: Crowd's Favourite (epic), Iron Greaves, Riposte and Big Lungs;
  - the respin/reroll token went unused.

## Read

- **It is fun and winnable.** The win came after about 25 runs across protos 9 to 13. Over that time the designer went from losing in bout 1 to reaching the champion regularly.
- **The rival works.** Beating the champion who had won four times is the moment the game is built around.
- **The proto 13 feint fix works,** with one read in the first run. More data is needed.
- **Perfect blocks are high (13 in one run).** The shield is now a strong, readable defence.
- **The decision: graduate from prototype mode.** Build the real v1 from proto 13, with the world sheet's richer reasons and background perks.

## The second win, minutes later

The designer: "I won again. This time it felt like there was a number of out-of-reach when enemies tried to hit, which surprised me. Also I'm actually not using the back and forth."

- **The numbers:**
  - 1 hit taken in five bouts;
  - **2 of 2 feints read**, both switch reads;
  - grades B, B, B, A, A;
  - picks: Lunge Master, Second Wind, and two epics (Mirror Shield and Lion's Heart);
  - the respin was spent on armour.
- **"Out of reach" 8 times, with 0 steps in or out and 64 lunges.** The player fights from lunge range and never steps. The foe starts attacks he cannot land, so his out-of-range swings are free turns for the player.
- **Read:**
  - **The foe must close before he commits.** He steps in until his weapon reaches, then winds up. "Out of reach" should only happen when the player backs off *during* his tell, which is a skill, so it should be rare.
  - **The step rocker is not used.** The lunge already does the stepping in, and the backstep dodge does the stepping out. v1 should simplify the left thumb, pending the designer's call.
  - **Difficulty may now be too low at the top.** After proto 13's feint fix and the richer deck, the run was won with one hit taken. v1 should look at the champion's tier and the epic odds, judged by the designer's next runs rather than by simulation.
