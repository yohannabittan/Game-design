# Playtest: Arena proto 13, the first win

- **Date:** 2026-10-08. Read from the designer's export and their note: "Wooooooo I managed to win."
- **Build:** arena-v15, dev channel, played in landscape (874x402).

## The winning run

- **The fighter:** Decimus-style scribe from Alexandria, M, STR 3, SPD 4, STA 4, medium armour (head, chest and legs plated), a spear, tier A. Reason: Glory.
- **The gauntlet:**
  - S dagger;
  - M axe;
  - L gladius;
  - S spear;
  - the champion, Marcus the Red, L gladius. He had beaten this designer in 4 earlier runs.
  
  **Rival defeated.**
- **The numbers:**
  - 13 slot hits and 4 punishes;
  - 25 blocks, 13 of them perfect;
  - 11 hits taken, against 6 half-hearts;
  - **the first feint ever read**: a switch read;
  - grades B, C, B, B, B;
  - picks: Crowd's Favourite (epic), Iron Greaves, Riposte and Big Lungs;
  - the respin/reroll token went unused.

## Read

- **It is fun and winnable.** The win came after about 25 runs across protos 9 to 13. Over that time the designer went from losing in bout 1 to reaching the champion regularly.
- **The rival works.** Beating the champion who had won four times is the moment the game is built around.
- **The proto 13 feint fix works,** with one read in the first run. More data is needed.
- **Perfect blocks are high (13 in one run).** The shield is now a strong, readable defence.
- **The decision: graduate from prototype mode.** Build the real v1 from proto 13, with the world sheet's richer reasons and background perks.
