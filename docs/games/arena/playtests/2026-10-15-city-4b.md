**Findings:**
1. **A losing streak earns no fame.** Fame pays for a win, a first clear, a thumbs up and every 50 gold left over. Four losses paid 0, because the gold goes on the tent. The meta helps least when the player most needs it.
2. **The crowd no longer maxes out, and may now be too hard to win.** After step 3.6 it ended at 30 to 42 and never reached the top third, even in the Showman run (Bread and Circuses). The Showman's cards and a thumbs up at the Grand Games both need the top third. Four lost runs are not enough to tell, since a win has more reads.
3. **The finisher button is still untested.** No war paint was picked in these four runs, so Momentum (5 to 10 a run) went unused again.
4. **The ludus was not used.** No trident or sica, no new origin and no veteran in these runs.
5. **The City went 0 for 4,** reaching bouts 2, 4, 3 and 3. The one earlier City run reached the champion.
6. **Spears are the top killer.** Across the 12 v2 losses, 7 were to a spear fighter, though about 2 fighters in 5 carry one.

Bleed works (62 damage in 266:37), and gold and the tent are used.
