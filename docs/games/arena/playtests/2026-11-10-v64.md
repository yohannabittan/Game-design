## Read

- **The knives were found and thrown, but none of them worked**, and nothing said why. The export can't tell a throw that hit plate from one that hit skin or missed.
- **Nothing teaches the throw.** The training yard doesn't cover knives (by design in Step 22).
- **What the throw asks for:**
  - tap the knife button;
  - then put the right thumb down and drag (the aim point sits 50 px above the thumb);
  - then let go.
  
  A tap on the foe throws at once, 50 px above the tap, which is likely why the throws missed.
- **The knife is drawn in the air above the head while raised,** not in the hand (an open item from v64).
