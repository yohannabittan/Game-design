**Findings:**
1. **No finisher ever fired.** Momentum spent is 0 in all 11 runs. Six of them held at least one finisher, with 7 to 15 Momentum gained. The long press on the sword thumb (still for 0.45 s, with no swing in the last 0.3 s) doesn't happen in a real fight, or isn't found.
2. **The crowd meter maxes out.** Both step 3.5 runs ended at 100. At this rate the Emperor's thumb would always be up, so it decides nothing.
3. **The difficulty is good:** 3 wins in 11, most losses at bouts 4 and 5, and feint reads at 1 to 3 of 5 per run. The builds are varied (bleed, Brute, Dancer and Wall), and gold and the tent are used in every run past bout 3.
