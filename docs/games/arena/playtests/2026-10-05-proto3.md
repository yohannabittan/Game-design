## Findings

- **It feels more like being a gladiator.**
- **The sword pose is weird:** the elbow is bent and it reads as a rapier. "He only attacks with a forward dash."
- **Spamming beats aiming.** "It's better to just attack, attack, attack than to really go for gaps." The data agrees: clangs run at 40 percent of all hits, and the score still climbs.
  - Causes: armour isn't punishing enough, the arm and the enemy's stance make any swing find something, and swings cost nothing.
- **Parry is too hard and dodge too easy:** 3 parries (1 perfect) against 23 dodges in 9 rounds. "Right now I want to always dodge."
- **Designer's fix: a shield.** A swipe forward raises the shield. A block costs shield durability. A perfect block costs none and opens a riposte.

## Proto 4 direction

1. A shield block on the forward swipe, with durability, and a perfect block that ripostes.
2. Dodge costs stamina and has a cooldown, and it backs you out of reach.
3. Swings cost stamina, and armour hits recoil hard enough to open you to a counter.
4. A natural sword arc around the shoulder, with the blade following the motion.
5. The enemy's guard covers parts, and openings come after their attacks.
