- **Date:** 2026-10-04, from the designer's export and notes. Viewport 402x812.

| Round | Input | Cuts | Clangs | Combos | Best combo | Dummies cleared |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 2 | offset | 31 | 9 | 8 | 3 | 13 |

## What it shows

- **Offset blade wins**, by the designer's preference and in the numbers. Over four offset rounds, cuts rose from 25 to 40 and combos from 4 to 13, while clangs fell from 20 to 8 to 14. That is a visible learning curve, so the skill ladder exists.
- **Slow motion** produced the most clangs (25): slower, but less precise.
- **Flick** produced the fewest combos.
- Cutting "feels satisfying in a way". The target stayed visible: the offset works.

## Designer direction for proto 2

"I control the weapon hand of my gladiator and can use it to try and hit the other gladiator, or I can swipe forward to block or parry, or maybe swipe back to dodge."

That means moving from an abstract drawn line to **direct control of the sword hand**: the blade follows the finger, with the offset, and every motion is the gladiator's arm.
