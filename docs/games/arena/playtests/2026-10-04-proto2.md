# Playtest: Arena proto 2 (the sword hand)

- **Date:** 2026-10-04, from the designer's notes.
- **Verdict:** "I've enjoyed this."

## Findings

- **The blade's weight is good.**
- **One very long arm doesn't feel like being the gladiator.** "In the full version I want my gladiator to look just like the other guy, and both be on screen, Swords and Sandals style."
- **Tells** are easy to read in time with a little practice.
- **Parry and dodge** feel distinct.

## Direction for proto 3

Two full-body gladiators on screen, side by side, facing each other in side view, the way Swords and Sandals frames its duels. Your finger still drives your gladiator's sword hand, with the offset and the weight, but now through a real arm from shoulder to elbow to hand. The opponent is a gladiator with the same tells, parts, armour and gaps as the dummy.
