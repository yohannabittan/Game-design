# Arena changelog

## v0.1 (unreleased)
- Created from skeleton.
- Layer 1 feel prototype (opus-free, sonnet-class builder, no review): straw dummy with seeded armour, drawn cut with hit-stop, straw burst, crunchy slice, clang sparks, combos; three input presets (offset blade, flick, slow motion) live in TUNE via cut.mode; 30 s round and result card. Choices: dummy resets with new armour as soon as every gap is cut (and at round end), body is 0.8 of screen width, a cut that runs past 0.6 s auto-strikes.
- Proto 2 feel prototype (sonnet, no review): direct sword hand with offset and weight (replaces the drawn cut); fast swings hit harder (3 damage tiers) and slow wipes do nothing; gap parts take hits then go dark (head stuns, arms slow the swing, chest and belly down fells the dummy); armour bounces with recoil and a hand lock, dents, breaks after 3; dummy winds up high, mid or low with a club and zone glow; swipe up in the last 0.3 s parries (first third perfect, riposte for double damage), swipe down dodges; 3 hearts, 45 s round, new result card; TUNE presets Gentle, Standard, Brutal.
- Proto 3 feel prototype (sonnet, no review, smoke only): landscape (manifest orientation landscape); two full-body side-view gladiators of one procedural build, yours on the left and the opponent on the right, each with seeded armour on 3 or 4 parts; your sword arm is a two-bone shoulder, elbow, hand chain that follows the finger with the offset and the weight (reach-limited, the body leans with the hand and steps in for the far arm); opponent wind-ups high, mid and low with a raised weapon arm, glow band and lunge; parry is a swipe right (your blade snaps to the clash point), dodge a swipe left (you hop back); hearts, round, result card (two columns) and TUNE as proto 2. `arena-v4`.
- Proto 4 feel prototype (sonnet, no review, smoke only): two thumbs (a touch that starts in the left third is the shield thumb); the sword arm is straight on a near-full-length circle round the shoulder at the finger's angle and bends only when the finger draws back, with a gladius whose angle follows the arm and the swing; overhead, jab (tip only) and slash come out of the motion with different hit rules, damage and recoil; stamina per attack (half damage at zero); a clang recoils hard and the foe counters at once; the foe guards one zone and opens for 0.8 s after each blow; left thumb swipe right raises a shield (4 blocks, perfect block in the last 0.15 s is free and ripostes), swipe left dodges (stamina, cooldown, out of reach until the thumb is held forward); result card adds the new counts; TUNE has the key numbers. `arena-v5`.
- Proto 5 feel prototype (sonnet, no review, smoke only): upright stance with the sword leading (lean at most 12 degrees, only on a fast slash past the arm's reach, no step-in), fighters closer and the hand reaches the legs; left-edge shield buttons High, Mid, Low (tap = 0.6 s, hold = keep up and drain stamina, only the matching zone blocks, the tell's button glows, a swipe back off the buttons stays the dodge); counters only after an overhead clang or two clangs in a row, with a 0.45 s tell; jab tip radius 10 px; "Turn your phone sideways" card in portrait; TUNE presets Forgiving, Standard, Brutal. `arena-v6`.
- Proto 6 feel prototype (sonnet, no review, smoke only): fighters stand apart (a 20 percent gap) and are framed larger; pushing the hand past your reach lunges you in a stride and back (extra stamina, no shield while lunging); dark joint slots (neck, armpit, waist, elbow, knee) are always open and deal 3x; bare parts take normal damage, plates protect and break but take no health; one health bar over the foe; 2 or 3 plates (legs always bare, never chest and belly both); counters only after an overhead clang (0.55 s glowing tell); Forgiving is the default preset; Dodge button below Low (tap 0.35 s sidestep, hold stays back and burns stamina; swipe dodge removed); zero stamina exhausts for 1.5 s (no swings, slow shield, flashing bar), faster regen when idle. `arena-v7`.
- Proto 7 feel prototype (sonnet, no review, smoke only): one hit per swing (the first thing the blade meets ends the swing); joint slots deal 3x only to a jab along the slit, a slash across a slot is a bare hit; foe health 14 to 26 (scaled by style); after a hit the foe slides a visible guard or shield disc over that spot for 3.2 s (down while he swings or is open) and steps in and out between blows; three seeded styles, one per foe in a shuffled order (Shield wall: big shield, slow, counters after any block; Duelist: small guard, fast tells, sidesteps a lunge; Brute: 4 plates, 2 slots, slow, blocking him drains stamina), name above the health bar. `arena-v8`.
- Proto 8 feel prototype (sonnet, no review, smoke only): the sword thumb works like a trackpad by default (TUNE: Sword control, Relative or Absolute): the hand is at a half-bent guard wherever the thumb lands, moves by the thumb's offset from the touch-down point times a gain of 1.6, and eases back to guard on lift; jab, slash and overhead are read from that motion, and a lunge needs a push of 1.3 arm lengths past the anchor (after the gain), so a resting thumb never lunges; a faint ring marks the anchor with a line to the thumb. `arena-v9`.
- Proto 9 feel prototype (opus, no review, smoke only): the foe fights with intent: per-weapon move sets with honest tells (chop, thrust, low sweep, a 2 or 3 hit combo that changes zones, a feint whose fake winds up shallower and never trembles, a shield bash for shield-bearers, a net throw for dagger and net), his own stamina bar and a winded state (1.5 s at zero, 1.4 s after any combo: guard down, 1.5x damage, "punish" label), footwork that keeps his weapon's range (spear long, dagger close) and backs off when hurt, a short memory (three blocks in one zone running make him break off and aim elsewhere; repeated lunges get sidestepped and punished; turtling brings feints and bashes; your low stamina makes him press), and exchanges of varied rhythm (flurry, probe, pause), all planned with the seeded fight RNG when the exchange starts; rolled fighters (size S/M/L drawn at scale, gladius, spear, axe or mace, dagger and net, light, medium or heavy armour, Strength, Speed and Stamina 1 to 5) with an intro card; a five-opponent gauntlet rising to a named champion, hearts carried over in halves with half a heart back per foe felled, no round timer; the end card shows each opponent's card and how far you got; the ledger line adds sizes, weapons, armour, stats, feints read, combos, winded counts, punishes, bashes, nets and reads. Open question taken simply: hits now take half a heart (heavy blows up to one and a half). `arena-v10`.
- Proto 10 feel prototype (opus, no review, smoke only): the feint is taught: the fake ends in a violet hesitation shimmer (zone band, weapon and shield button) before the glow jumps to the real zone, the first feint ever runs at 0.4 speed with the caption "FEINT! He faked HIGH. Wait for the glow to settle, then block where it lands.", the second shows a short reminder (counted in the save as `feintLesson`); "Feint read!" (no shield or dodge through the fake, then a block of the real strike) drains 60 percent of his stamina bar and is the only thing `feintsRead` counts now; the ramp: bout 1 is a sparring partner (no feints, no memory, no counters, tells 40 percent longer, stats centred on 1, "Sparring: learn his tells." on the intro card), feints from bout 2, memory from bout 3; the open window: when he is winded, 0.15 s of quarter-speed time, "OPEN!", his slots glow, and the 1.5x applies after rounding so a punish is exactly 1.5x; your fighter is rolled at setup on five weighted wheels with their odds and tier names (size, Strength, Speed, Stamina, armour; 0.8 s each, tap to stop), then a "Your fighter" card with a tier letter S to D and an underdog bonus (+1 half heart below a score of 7, about 4 percent of rolls); size sets body scale, reach, hearts (2.5, 3, 3.5) and swing speed, Strength sword damage, Speed hand lag, recoil and dodge length, Stamina bar and regen, armour plates head, chest or legs (heavy adds the shield arm) and slows regen, and a blow on a plate costs half (a quarter heart for a light blow); you are drawn at your size with your plates, and your card leads the end card; the ledger adds your size, stats, armour, plates, tier, underdog, plate saves and a bout-1 loss flag. Open question taken simply: every hit in the window still does 1.5x, as in proto 9. `arena-v11`.
- Proto 11 feel prototype (opus, no review, smoke only): the fighters are skinned, drawn over the same hit shapes: shaped plates with a bevelled rim, highlight and shadow edges, rivets and leather straps (a curved muscle cuirass, banded belly lames, a segmented manica, thigh plates and greaves with a knee boss, helmets with brims, visor grilles and crests), dents as dark dimples with a scratch and cracks as jagged lines; joint slots are gaps between two plate edges with padded linen or skin and a strap inside, in shadow, a warm rim light, and a glint when your tip is near; shaded skin with muscle lines, tunics or loincloths with folds, belts and sandals; the weapon sets the type (gladius murmillo: fish-crest helmet, rectangular shield; spear hoplomachus: plumed helmet, small round bronze shield; axe or mace brute: open helmet, heavy pauldron; dagger and net retiarius: iron cap at most, a bronze galerus, the net hanging from the belt); your look follows your armour set (light bronze and leather, medium steel with bronze trim, heavy polished steel with gold and a red crest); a bare hit leaves a small red cut that fades over 3.5 s. Each part is painted once into an offscreen sprite and repainted only on a dent, crack, break or damage step, and blitted unsmoothed (smoothed blits cost about 25 percent frame time in software). Open questions taken simply: a helmet is drawn only on a plated head (a bare murmillo or hoplomachus wears a leather cap with his crest, a bare brute or retiarius shows his face), so the art never makes a bare part look armoured; the brute's pauldron is iron only when his weapon arm is plated (studded leather otherwise); the legs no longer sway separately. `arena-v12`.
- Proto 12 feel prototype (opus, no review, smoke only): the upgrade draft: after each fell, before the next intro card, the bout is graded (S: no hits taken and at least 2 feint reads plus punishes; A: at most 1 hit and 3 slot hits, reads and punishes; B: at most 3 hits and 1; else C) and three seeded cards are dealt with the grade's rarity odds shown (C 80/18/2 up to S 30/45/25, an S always deals one epic first); all 13 cards work (Thick Hide, Second Wind, Quick Wrist, Steady Shield, Sandal Grip, Iron Greaves, Bronze Helm, Heavy Arm, Keen Eye, Riposte, Champion's Blade, Crowd's Favourite, Unbreakable), icons sit under the hearts and are listed on the end card; footwork: the Dodge button is a back / in rocker of the same size (Back: tap is the old dodge as a real backstep, hold keeps backing off and burns stamina; In: tap a pace, hold to walk in), the arena has a timber wall behind each fighter and "Back to the wall!" when you can give no more ground, a blow lands only within his striking distance plus a little reach ("OUT OF REACH" otherwise), he keeps his weapon's distance from where you stand at a walking speed, barely moves while winding up, stands still while winded, and is driven back half a pace by a perfect block or a blocked bash (his bash drives you back the same); your story: Origin and Reason wheels after the stat wheels, a rolled name, "Gaius, a baker from Capua. Fights for: Freedom." on the fighter card, Old Brutus's PRD lines on every intro card, the draft and the end card, a rival saved when the champion cuts you down who returns as the next run's champion with his taunt ("Rival defeated!" clears him), an epilogue card when the champion falls, and otherwise the epitaph and a pit wall of the last 8 fallen names; feint reads loosened (coordinator, from the proto 11 export): a read is now any block of the real strike, so raising the fake zone and switching counts, and every feint ends as exactly one of read, bitten or neither (all three logged); save v2 (rival, wall, with migrate); the ledger adds grades, picks, steps in and out, wall hits (yours and his), out-of-reach whiffs, shrugs, origin, reason, rival and rivalBeaten. Open questions taken simply: commons stack while rares and epics are dealt once (never a plate you already wear); Heavy Arm doubles an overhead's dents (it already dented twice); Riposte's counter jab is an automatic riposte-damage jab into his first bare part; Unbreakable ignores the first blow that would hurt you; the rocker's halves keep the old button's size but their touch areas reach past its edges to stay 44 px wide; the epilogue and epitaph say "woman" and "her" for Lucia, Aurelia and Tullia. `arena-v13`.
- Proto 12.1 feel prototype (sonnet, no review, smoke only): bigger left-thumb buttons: the column is 22 percent of the screen width (never under 96 px) with High, Mid and Low sharing the height above the step row (6 px apart, about 72 px tall at 390), and the rocker is now two separate big buttons, back and in, side by side at the bottom (each half the column, at least 64 px tall), whose touch areas reach 8 px past their drawn edges but never into a neighbour, and a press that slides from one to the other switches; the fighters' frame slides right to keep their gap clear of the column; labels scale with the buttons; the sizes live in `TUNING.col`. `arena-v14`.
