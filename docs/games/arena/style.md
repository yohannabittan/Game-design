# Arena style anchor

Prepend verbatim to every image prompt for this game:

"Bold painted illustration in the style of a modern 2D mobile fighting game: warm Roman arena colours (sand, terracotta, bronze, deep red, olive), clean dark outlines of even weight, soft cel shading with one highlight and one shadow tone, slightly heroic proportions, readable silhouettes, no gore, no blood, no photorealism, no text unless asked."

## Rules for this game

- **Fighters are drawn by the game on a rig** (a body made of parts that pivot at the joints). Painted fighter art is a cut-out kit: one image per body part, painted to fit that part's rectangle, with the joints at the ends. The hitboxes, the gaps and the plates stay the game's own drawing, on top of the paint (ADR-0021).
- **Backdrops sit behind the sand** and must stay soft and low in contrast in the fight band (the middle of the image), so the fighters and their tells always read first.
