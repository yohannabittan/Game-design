# Arena step: a push you mean as a jab lands as a jab (locked 2026-11-17)

**The designer:** "Go on the Jab fix."

**What was found** (two-weapon build and its verifier, 2026-11-14; `../playtests/2026-11-14-evening-five.md`):
- With a spear (one or two), a straight thumb push is often classed as a SLASH, not a jab: the blade's angle skews the stroke's measured direction. The designer's two spear runs: 83 slashes to 22 jabs.
- The axe's jab clears `attack.jab.minSpeed` (520) by 0.4% on a clean fresh touch (522). A touch that starts during a hit-stop peaks at about 503 and is lost. With one axe, a hurried push at no rest lands 1 of 6.
- With two daggers, a push never reaches the jab gate (peak 474).
- With one weapon, a hurried push lands rarely on the trident and spear (4 of 6).

## What the player gets

- Push your thumb straight at him and you jab, with any weapon, one or two, at any body size: it never comes out as a slash, and it never comes out as nothing.
- A sideways or arcing stroke is still a slash, and a raise that turns down is still an overhead: nothing that is a slash or overhead today turns into a jab.
- What a jab does when it lands (damage, gaps, plate, the spear's slide) is unchanged.
