# Arena step: the fall scenes, when you lose (draft, 2026-11-21)

**The designer** (2026-10-26): "When you die nothing happens, but it would be sweet to have something like either you're spared, or the guy cuts your head off and shows it to the crowd, or an animal jumps on you, makes you fall and eats you." (2026-11-21): "We're still missing the death animations, remember." Planned since as "the fall scenes: spared, executed or mauled, by crowd favour and foe type" (`../prd-v0.3.md`).

## What the player gets

When your last heart goes, a short scene (a few seconds, skippable with a tap) instead of nothing:
- **Spared:** the crowd loved you (favour high): you drop to a knee, the Emperor's thumb goes up, the crowd roars; you live to fight again (the run still ends).
- **Executed:** the crowd is cold: you drop to your knees; he raises his blade over you; the Emperor's thumb goes down; the blow falls (cut away at the moment of the blow, in the game's comic-violent tone: no gore lingered on), and he raises his weapon to the crowd.
- **Mauled:** a beast felled you: it leaps, you go down under it, the sand flies, the crowd gasps.
Then the run's end as today. The Emperor's thumb, the crowd and the beasts' leap already exist in the game.
