# Arena step: helmets that show the fighter, and a crest that faces the right way (drafted 2026-11-14)

**The designer:** "There's a crest of feathers for a helmet, it seems, that shows the wrong way. Also I'm not the biggest fan of the full coverage helmet, and I think we should rework them. Maybe we can use some inspiration from the classic gladiator movies' gladiator helms."

**Today:** four helmets hide the whole face (the murmillo's and the thraex's grilles, the secutor's smooth dome with two holes, the phalanx helm), plus the dark hood. A fighter wearing one has no face, so no expression and no character.

**The goal:** a helmeted fighter is still a person you can read: you see eyes, or the face, and the helmet looks like something from a gladiator film, heavy and dramatic.

**How we get there (sketch before build):**
1. **A probe on one helmet,** the murmillo: three directions painted onto the head (art Pack 31), for the designer to pick from.
2. **The picked direction is applied to the other closed helmets** (thraex, secutor, phalanx helm), keeping each one's silhouette and crest so the types still read apart.
3. **A wiring build** swaps the paintings in. The helmets become open ones in the code (`closed` off where the face shows), so the face gap and expressions work under them.

**The crest:** find which crest or plume sweeps toward the face instead of back from it, on you (facing right) and on a foe (facing left), and flip it. A crest always trails away from the face.

**Not copied from any film.** The look is the general one of film gladiators: no specific film's helmet is reproduced (the repo is public).

**The designer will judge it** by the contact sheet of the probe, then by a frame of a bout with the new helmets.
