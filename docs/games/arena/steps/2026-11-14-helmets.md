# Arena step: retire the two-eyehole helmet, and turn one crest the right way (locked 2026-11-14)

**The designer,** first: "There's a crest of feathers for a helmet, it seems, that shows the wrong way. Also I'm not the biggest fan of the full coverage helmet." Then, after seeing the helmets again in the v69 GIFs: "I think the helmets pretty much all look good now, except the fully metal helmet with only the two eyeholes. I'd like to retire that one."

**So the rework is off.** The grilled helmets and the phalanx helm stay as they are. The art probe (Pack 31) was stopped before it made anything.

**1. Retire the secutor's helmet** (`kit-helmet-secutor`: the smooth metal dome with two eyeholes).
- No fighter wears it: not you, not a foe, not a champion, not the inventory doll.
- Whoever rolled it gets another of the kit's helmets instead, chosen so that fighters still look different from each other. A saved fighter or rival who wore it loads wearing the replacement.
- Its painting leaves the game's files and the offline list. The art stays in `docs/art/final/arena/`.

**2. The crests that face the wrong way** (found 2026-11-14: `2026-11-14-crest-finding.md`, sheet `../sketches/helmets-crests.png`).
- They are four of your Tailor's looks, on you only: the red plume and the white plume (the tuft sweeps forward over the face), and the bronze and silver crests (tall end at the front).
- The black plume, the gold crest, every helmet and every foe are already right.
- Turn those four so they trail back like their siblings, staying seated on the head where they sit now. A crest always trails back, away from the face.

**The designer will judge it** by a frame of fighters in a bout: no two-eyehole dome anywhere, and a frame of you in each of the six Tailor crests and plumes, every one trailing back.
