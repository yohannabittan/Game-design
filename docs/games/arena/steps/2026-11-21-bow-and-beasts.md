# Arena step: the bow against beasts, aimed like the knife, and the bow trials (locked 2026-11-21)

**The designer:** "I think having the bow to help with animals by making animals running from far would be good. And also bow trials." Earlier: "I think we need to talk about the bow, which I haven't managed to make work." (17 s with the bow out and no arrow loosed: raising it makes him rush in at 1.8x, a shot inside his reach is refused as TOO CLOSE, and a release before 0.6 s as TOO SOON.)

## What the player gets

- **Beasts charge from far:** a beast bout (the Colosseum's lion and bear, young and grown, and the Beastmaster's lion) starts with the beast at the far end of the sand; it charges. With a bow you get a few shots as it comes; then steel. Without one, the charge is something to dodge or block, as a beast's first blow.
- **The bow aims like the knife:** one press, slide up or down to set the angle (a dotted line), let go to loose. Holding longer draws it further and adds power; a short draw still fires, weaker. **It always fires**: up close a shot is weaker, never refused, and every shot says what it hit.
- **Against men** the bow keeps its place as a sidearm, but a man closes at his normal pace, not a sprint.
- **The bow trials** (Step 27b, `../prd-v0.3.md`): **Shrinking Target: bow** at near, mid and far marks, and **The Apple Shot**: a man at the far mark with fruit on his head, a pumpkin down to an apple (Pack 30's fruits and fruit seller), the fruit bursts, hit the man and it ends (the comic OW!), he gets nervous from the third fruit.

## Decided (2026-11-21, after the sketch)

**The designer:** "Yes, the animal should run over before the fight begins and you can shoot it with bow or knives."

- **The charge is the opening of every beast bout:** the beast starts at the far end of the sand and runs at you before the fight proper begins. You can shoot it with the bow **or throw knives at it** as it comes; each hit staggers and slows it (as on the sketch page), enough hits can drop it before it reaches you. If it reaches you, the fight goes on as today from there.
- **The bow's feel is the sketch's defaults** (`../sketches/bow-aim.html`): CHARGE normal (about 2.5 s from the far end, speeding up), arrows drop at range with the dotted line showing the real flight, a longer draw adds power (tick at 0.6 s, full at 1.2 s), a quick release is a weak shot, never refused. The sketch is the reference for how it should feel under the thumb.
- **Knives against the charge** aim as they do today against men.
- Everything else as above: men close at normal pace when the bow is out; the two bow trials on the Range tab.
