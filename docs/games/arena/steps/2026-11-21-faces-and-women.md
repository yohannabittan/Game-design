# Arena step: faces by origin, and women who look like women (draft, 2026-11-21)

**The designer:** "Let's do more faces and women's bodies, let's up the art budget, and please remember the mistakes we made in the past to make sure the women's bodies are correct."

- **Today:** one face for every fighter; women fighters (any origin can roll a woman's name; Sparta's gladiatrix always) are drawn as the man without his beard. Mevia the Huntress likewise.
- **What the player gets:** your fighter's face comes from their origin (a man's or a woman's face for each, Sparta's women only); women have a woman's body in every build (lean, average, muscular) with the same kit and the same armour; foes get the same variety, and Mevia looks like a woman.
- **Helmets:** worn helmets are painted with a face inside, so a fighter's own face shows bare-headed and in open helmets; closed helmets keep today's worn painting.
- **Art first:** Art Pack 32's successor, Pack 34 (`docs/art/BRIEF.md`): a probe of one gladiatrix assembled on the rig and six faces, for the designer's eye, before the full set. The wiring is a build after the art lands.
- **Budget:** raised to $100 (`docs/assets-ledger.md`).

## Added 2026-11-22: torsos turned to the profile, and shoulders that hold

**The designer:** "The chest plates are head on, but the character's face and arms act as if we're seeing it from the side; we need to change the chest pieces to match." And: "Sometimes there are arm shoulder dislocations, like on the home page" (the menu fighter with the axe raised overhead: the upper arm leaves the torso's painted shoulder, with a gap between them). Both are the shoulder joint of a frontal torso under a side-on arm. The art is Pack 34's second probe (`docs/art/BRIEF.md`); the wiring build that follows must show, at 4x and at game size, the near shoulder with the arm at rest, in guard, raised overhead (the menu pose, an overhead strike), swung and thrown, for every build, bare and in plate, and the menu fighter in particular: one arm joined to one shoulder in every pose.
