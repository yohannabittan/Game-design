# Arena step: your spear is as long as his (locked 2026-11-14)

**The designer,** after a run with two spears: "The range of the spear felt weirdly short." Shown the measurement: "Fix spear please."

**What was found** (`2026-11-14-spear-reach-finding.md`, still `../sketches/spear-yours-vs-his.png`): your spear is drawn about half as long as a foe's and reaches only about 12% past a sword, while a spear foe stands off by twice that. Your jab falls about 60 px short of a spearman at 874x402.

## What the player gets

**Equal bodies carry equal spears.**
- Your spear is drawn as long as a foe's spear on a body of the same size, held the way yours is held today.
- It reaches as far as it is drawn: the hit still lands where the point is.
- Standing where a spear foe stands off to strike you, a medium fighter's jab reaches him without a lunge, just as his thrust reaches you.
- A lean or runt body still carries everything shorter, and a big one longer, as now. That trade stays.

**The trident stays the longest weapon,** longer than the spear by about the share it is today.

**Two spears:** the off-hand spear is drawn the same length as the main one.

**Nothing else moves:** foes' weapons and where they stand, every other weapon of yours, damage, speed and price.

## Acceptance

1. At 874x402, a medium fighter's spear at rest is drawn within 10% of a medium spear foe's, measured fist to point.
2. From a medium spear foe's stand-off distance, a medium fighter's level spear jab lands on him, with no lunge. A lean fighter's falls short by no more than 20 px.
3. Against a swordsman, a spear jab lands from further off than a gladius jab does, by at least 60 px for a medium fighter.
4. At full lunge with a spear, and with a trident, both fighters and both weapons' points are on screen at 874x402 and at 667x375, in the open and with either fighter at the wall. The left column covers neither point.
5. The weapon's card in the purse and the inventory shows the longer spear, not clipped.
6. The training yard still teaches with a spear: its ghost strokes land, and the yard checks pass.
7. `npm run check arena` passes. A permanent check holds items 2 and 3. Any replay whose hash rightly changes is named in the report for the orchestrator to accept.
8. `RULES.md` says the new reach, and its "Two blades" line is corrected: you can buy a second copy of any weapon (the gladius-and-dagger limit is for foes).

## Open questions, simplest option taken

- **The rest pose:** your spear points up about 35 degrees at rest. Left as it is; only the length changes.
- **Balance:** a longer spear is a stronger pick. Not retuned here. The designer's play decides whether it needs a cost.
- **The off hand's follow-up with two weapons:** unchanged; two weapons is being redesigned from sketches (`../sketches/dual-*.gif`).

## The designer will judge it

By a still of you with a spear beside a spear foe, and by playing a spear run.
