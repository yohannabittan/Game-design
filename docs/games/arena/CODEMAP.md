# Arena code map

Line ranges are as of the v1 feature build (3152 lines, `arena-v17`) and drift as `games/arena/src/game.js` changes. The anchors are the stable part: run `node tools/codemap-check.mjs arena` to warn when one disappears. Find a section by its anchor with `grep -n "NAME" games/arena/src/game.js`. Each top-level section opens with a `// ====== NAME ======` line, and sub-sections with `// ----- name -----`.

## To change X, read Y

1. **Retune the sword feel.** Read `TUNING.control`, `hand`, `arm`, `blade`, `lunge` and `attack`, then `moveHand` (the hand following the thumb), `updateAttack` (how a jab, slash or overhead is named) and `poseYou` (the lunge).
2. **Change what a hit does.** Read `checkHits`, `alongSlit`, `bounce` and `gapHit` (damage, the weapon and slot multipliers, the punish), then `TUNING.attack`, `armour`, `part`, `juice` and `me.weapons`.
3. **Change how he attacks or moves.** Read `planExchange`, `buildMove`, `TUNING.moves`, `rhythm` and `weapons[].moves`, then `updateFoe` (the swing clock and the commit rule, `inReach`), `stepFoe` and `chaseIn` (his range; `TUNING.step` and `foot`) and `poseFoe` (the tell's look).
4. **Change what happens when his blow lands.** Read `resolveSwing` (your dodge, range, block, perfect block, feint read, net, bash, upgrades, plates), then `TUNING.shield` and `foot.reach`.
5. **Change the feint and its lesson.** Read `buildMove` (the feint branch), `startBlow`, `feintHesitate`, `feintSwitch`, `feintRead`, `settleFeint`, `TUNING.moves.feint` and `lesson`; the look is `drawZone` and `drawShieldBtns`.
6. **Change the left thumb's buttons.** Read `TUNING.col`, `colRect`, `shieldBtns`, `hitRect`, `buttonAt`, `raiseShield`, `dodge`, `stepYou`, and `play.onPointerDown` / `onPointerMove` / `onPointerUp`; the look is `drawShieldBtns` and `drawDodgeBtn`.
7. **Change your fighter's roll or the gauntlet.** Read `TUNING.me`, `ME_WHEEL`, `rollMe`, `deriveMe`, `respinMe`, `WHEELS` and `drawMeCard`; for the foes, `GAUNTLET`, `rollFighter`, `rollGauntlet`, `TUNING.sizes`, `weapons` and `armourSets`, and `newRack`.
8. **Change the story text.** Read `ORIGINS`, `PERKS`, `MY_NAMES`, `BRUTUS`, `BRUTUS_BG`, `REVENGE`, `CHAMP_TIE`, `CHAMP_LINE`, `brutusIntro`, `brutusWon`, `EPILOGUE`, `storyLine`, `tauntOf`, `STORY` and `TUNING.story`; they show in `drawIntro`, `drawThread`, `drawDraft`, `drawMeCard`, `menu` and `over`. A perk acts where `bg()` is read.
9. **Add or change an upgrade.** Read `DECK`, `TUNING.cards`, `dealable`, `pickCard`, the `has('id')` or `count('id')` call where it acts, and `drawIcon` for its icon; grades and odds are `gradeBout` and `TUNING.grade` and `draft`.
10. **Change the save, the ledger or the end card.** Read `play.finish` (the ledger line, the rival, the wall), `over`, and `game` (`saveVersion`, `migrate`); save keys are `rival`, `wall`, `feintLesson`, `boutsSeen`, `best2` and `run` (written by `saveRun` at each bout's start from `RUN_KEYS`, read by `resumeRun`).
11. **Change the look of a fighter.** Read `GTYPE`, `myLook`, `lookOf`, the `paint*` functions and `spriteOf` (its cache key), then `drawFighter`, `drawSwordArm` and `drawShield`.
