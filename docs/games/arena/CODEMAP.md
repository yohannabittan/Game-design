# Arena code map

Line ranges are as of the v2 step 3a build (4609 lines, `arena-v20`) and drift as `games/arena/src/game.js` changes. The anchors are the stable part: run `node tools/codemap-check.mjs arena` to warn when one disappears. Find a section by its anchor with `grep -n "NAME" games/arena/src/game.js`. Each top-level section opens with a `// ====== NAME ======` line, and sub-sections with `// ----- name -----`.

## To change X, read Y

1. **Retune the sword feel.** Read `TUNING.control`, `hand`, `arm`, `blade`, `lunge` and `attack`, then `moveHand` (the hand following the thumb), `updateAttack` (how a jab, slash or overhead is named) and `poseYou` (the lunge).
2. **Change what a hit does.** Read `checkHits`, `alongSlit`, `bounce` and `gapHit` (damage, the weapon and slot multipliers, the punish), then `TUNING.attack`, `armour`, `part`, `juice` and `me.weapons`.
3. **Change how he attacks or moves.** Read `planExchange`, `buildMove`, `TUNING.moves`, `rhythm` and `weapons[].moves`, then `updateFoe` (the swing clock and the commit rule, `inReach`), `stepFoe` and `chaseIn` (his range; `TUNING.step` and `foot`) and `poseFoe` (the tell's look).
4. **Change what happens when his blow lands.** Read `resolveSwing` (your dodge, range, block, perfect block, feint read, net, bash, upgrades, plates), then `TUNING.shield` and `foot.reach`.
5. **Change the feint and its lesson.** Read `buildMove` (the feint branch), `startBlow`, `feintHesitate`, `feintSwitch`, `feintRead`, `settleFeint`, `TUNING.moves.feint` and `lesson`; the look is `drawZone` and `drawShieldBtns`.
6. **Change the left thumb's buttons.** Read `TUNING.col`, `colRect`, `shieldBtns`, `hitRect`, `buttonAt`, `raiseShield`, `dodge`, `stepYou`, and `play.onPointerDown` / `onPointerMove` / `onPointerUp`; the look is `drawShieldBtns` and `drawDodgeBtn`.
7. **Change your fighter's roll or the gauntlet.** Read `TUNING.me`, `ME_WHEEL`, `rollMe`, `deriveMe`, `respinMe`, `WHEELS` and `drawMeCard`; for the foes, `GAUNTLET`, `rollFighter`, `rollGauntlet`, `TUNING.sizes`, `weapons` and `armourSets`, and `newRack`.
8. **Change the story text.** Read `ORIGINS`, `PERKS`, `MY_NAMES`, `BRUTUS`, `BRUTUS_BG`, `REVENGE`, `CHAMP_TIE`, `CHAMP_LINE`, `brutusIntro`, `brutusWon`, `EPILOGUE`, `storyLine`, `tauntOf`, `STORY` and `TUNING.story`; they show in `drawIntro`, `drawThread`, `drawDraft`, `drawMeCard`, `menu` and `over`. A perk acts where `bg()` is read.
9. **Add or change a card.** Read `DECK` (its family, tier, `needs`, `bans`), `TUNING.cards` (or `TUNING.fin` for a finisher), then where it acts: a hook in `HOOKS` (the events are listed above `HOOKS`, fired by `emit`), or a `has('id')` check in hit resolution; a finisher needs a `FIN_OK` entry and a branch in `fireFinisher`. Dealing is `dealable`, `dealDraft` and `TUNING.draft`; a side effect on picking (Titan's hearts) is `applyCard`. Check it in the card lab (`drawLab`, dev channel).
10. **Change Momentum, Bleed or crippling.** Read `TUNING.mom`, `bleed` and `lame`, then `gainMom`, `addBleed`, `updateBleed`, `tellDodge`, `disable` (a laming), `lamed` and where it acts (`startBlow`, `stepFoe`, the cover slide in `play.update`, `poseFoe`); the look is `drawPips`, `foeHealth`, `markLamed` and `lameIcon`.
11. **Change gold, the tent or prices.** Read `TUNING.gold`, `tent` and `draft.price`, then `boutGold`, `fell`, `priceOf`, `pickCard`, `openTent`, `buyWare` and `WARES`; the look is `drawDraft` and `drawTent`.
12. **Change the save, the ledger or the end card.** Read `play.finish` (the ledger line, the rival, the wall), `over`, and `game` (`saveVersion`, `migrate`); save keys are `rivals` (per arena), `arenas` (the highest open), `wall`, `feintLesson`, `boutsSeen`, `best2` and `run` (with its `arena`) (written by `saveRun` at each bout's start, when a draft opens and when the tent opens, from `RUN_KEYS`, read by `resumeRun`).
13. **Change the look of a fighter.** Read `GTYPE`, `myLook`, `lookOf`, the `paint*` functions and `spriteOf` (its cache key), then `drawFighter`, `drawSwordArm` and `drawShield`.
14. **Add or change a piece of gear, a set or a gap guard.** Read `DECK` (`slot`, `gear`, `tier` as rarity), `gearOf` and `applyCard` (one piece a slot), then its painter (`GEAR_PAINT` and the `paint*Gear` functions, `paintCloak`, `paintGuard`; the shield and weapon pieces in `drawShield`, `drawFoeShield`, `weaponGear`, `liveArmGear`) and `withGear` (the sprite key). His gear and guards are `rollGear`, `rollGuards`, `TUNING.foeGear` and `guard`; what his pieces do is `foeHas` in `bounce`, `gapHit` and `resolveSwing`. The deal is `dealDraft`, `famPick`, `cascadeTiers` and `TUNING.draft.cascade`.
15. **Change an arena, the pairs or the crowd.** Read `ARENAS` (rows, champion pools, Brutus's line), `TUNING.arenas` (shift, feints, guards, gear, ring, the pairs bout), `rollGauntlet` and `rollFighter`; the pairs are `TUNING.pairs`, `makeFoe`, `updatePair`, `stepIn`, `fellOne` and the fall clock in `play.update`, drawn by `drawEdge`, `drawEdgeTag` and `drawPairIntro`. The crowd is `TUNING.crowd`, `favour`, `crowdEvent` (called from `emit`), `crowdDrain`, `coins` and `drawCrowdMeter`; the endings are `endingOf`, `bandOf` and `over.thumb`; unlocks are `play.finish` (`arenas`) and `pickArena`.
