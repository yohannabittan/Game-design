# Recoil changelog

## v0.1 (unreleased)
- Created from skeleton.
- Layer 1 mechanic built by Sonnet (review pending): landscape 640x360, two thumbs, recoil rule, Accuracy 1-3 and Speed 1-3 with seeded targets, stars from 85/55/30 percent of the scripted perfect run, points and card. Section 17 choices: drag zone is the left half of the screen; Accuracy targets stay until `accLife` (a miss shot spends ammo but the target stays). Tuning change: `kickPerShot` 7 to 8, because at 7 two shots 0.1 s apart differ by 21.9 units at x = 400 (below the 24-unit acceptance check) and at 8 by 27.7. Additions outside the PRD: a Menu button in the HUD, Play on the menu picks the first unlocked challenge with no stars, a red left-edge flash when a target reaches the line.

## v0.2 (unreleased)
- Built by Sonnet (review pending): review fixes and the v0.2 delta together. Review fixes: Menu button moved top-right beside the score, combo pips and multiplier moved to the ground band (out of the range-finder band, gunMinY unchanged); `pickY` now builds a legal interval and, if the band cannot allow `minDy`, takes the farthest point (Accuracy 3 places its low and high targets so every change is at least 70, Speed 3 uses minDy 50 because its band is 106 tall); the card ignores taps for 0.4 s; fire on touch down on the right half (hold fires only for auto guns); space through `onKey` (key repeat fires at the gun's rate); portrait shows a "Rotate your phone" banner over the ground band and stays playable.
- v0.2: gun profiles as data (Service pistol, Carbine, chosen on the menu and saved), accuracy sets the range finder length with exact shots, carbine fires on hold; barrel sway from gun movement (swayPerSpeed, swayMax, swayRecovery, measured over a 0.05 s window), added to the kick and shown on the range finder; weave, dodge, horde, skeet and boss behaviours; Accuracy 4, Speed 3 with weave, Speed 4, Skeet 1 and 2, Boss 1; four ladders on the menu; presets Fair, Twitchy, Lazy and four sliders (weaveAmp, skeetSpeed, carbine accuracy, swayPerSpeed). Save version 2, `migrate` keeps existing bests and adds the gun choice.
- Choices where the PRD is silent: a dodged shot is neutral (spends ammo, keeps the combo); dodge flickers while armed and for `dodgeWarn` 0.4 s before it re-arms; a skeet or horde target that is not shot is a miss and resets the combo; magSize and reloadSeconds are carried in the profile but unused (Accuracy keeps its ammo, the other ladders are unlimited); the old kickPerShot, kickRecovery, dragGain and approachSpeed experiments are dropped because the tune panel fits four sliders in landscape (kick is per gun now); the old kick, drag and approach experiments were dropped from the tune panel.
- Fire-rate conflict, resolved in the PRD: with the first build's pistol fireRate 4 (0.25 s between taps) equal to its kick recovery time (8 degrees at 32 per second) the kick could never stack, so the v0.1 "second quick shot lands 24 units higher" check could not pass. The PRD and code are now pistol fireRate 9 and carbine kickRecovery 24 (rule: a gun's fire interval must be shorter than kickPerShot / kickRecovery).
- Star thresholds by the v0.1 rule from scripted pistol runs (perfect scores): Accuracy 4 2950, Speed 4 3630, Skeet 1 2950, Skeet 2 3750, Boss 1 3750; existing challenges unchanged.
- Engine change (ADR-0014, done by the orchestrator): the tune panel restores only declared keys (sliders plus preset keys, so Twitchy and Lazy now persist) and prunes stale saved keys; Reset restores every declared key. This corrects the earlier note that preset values did not persist.
- Review round 2 (Sonnet): a hit on an inactive boss part is now neutral (no points, no damage, no combo change, not a hit; the shot is spent), so it cannot be farmed; saveVersion 3 and `migrate` prunes saved tune values to the declared keys; the horde spacing and jitters, skeet pair split and offset, launch spread and core x moved from `build` into the challenge data; a horde breaching together plays the miss sound and buzz once per 0.3 s; `gunMinY` is 60 (PRD v0.1 amended) so the Menu button never covers the range finder; the rotate hint is drawn in the letterbox below the field; the tap gate allows one step of slack, so a tap at the nominal interval is not dropped (shortest gap now 0.1083 s, 9.23 per second for the pistol; held auto fire keeps the exact rate).

- A. Stars come from noisy bots. A bot fires with every shot's barrel angle off by a Gaussian of sigma degrees (kick compensated, dodgers probed, the Bunker played by its state-search policy), 100 seeds, sigma from 0 to 5 in 0.25 steps; the scores are stored as curves in `BARS` (mean `m` and 25th percentile `q` per sigma) and a bar is read off them at the noise TUNE sets, so a slider needs no re-run. Three stars are the mean at 0.75 degrees, two stars the mean at 1.5, one star the 25th percentile at 3.0 (see decisions); a bar never exceeds 95 percent of the perfect run (`starCap`), so perfect play always earns three stars (only Boss 1 with the shotgun needed it: its 0.75 degree mean, 1006, is above its perfect 1000); bars round to 10 and stay strictly rising. The gun is the challenge's `bestGun`: the pistol on Accuracy and Skeet, the carbine on Speed, each gun's own on the bosses. The shotgun is not the Speed 4 and 5 gun because a fan makes its score independent of noise (3240, 3256 and 3227 at 0.75, 1.5 and 3 degrees), so it cannot set a bar. Presets Pro (0.5 / 1.0 / 2.0), Skilled (0.75 / 1.5 / 3.0, the default) and Casual (1.0 / 2.0 / 4.0) and three sliders are on TUNE, which is now the game's own screen (the engine's holds two sliders; this has a Star bars panel and a Handling panel side by side, and shows the bars it gives on Accuracy 3 and Speed 3). Saved stars are never revoked, and the stars shown for a saved best are the saved stars or what its score earns against the bars now, whichever is more (a boss best does not record its gun, so it is read against the pistol's bars); points and unlocks follow the shown stars. Save version 6 adds `starPreset` (the preset's name, or Custom); `migrate` sets Skilled. The card says which bars it used. The bars assume the default handling (pistol kick 8, sway 0.02); if a Handling preset is kept they are a little off, and they must be regenerated whenever the sim changes (section B will change it).
