# Recoil changelog

## v0.1 (unreleased)
- Created from skeleton.
- Layer 1 mechanic built by Sonnet (review pending): landscape 640x360, two thumbs, recoil rule, Accuracy 1-3 and Speed 1-3 with seeded targets, stars from 85/55/30 percent of the scripted perfect run, points and card. Section 17 choices: drag zone is the left half of the screen; Accuracy targets stay until `accLife` (a miss shot spends ammo but the target stays). Tuning change: `kickPerShot` 7 to 8, because at 7 two shots 0.1 s apart differ by 21.9 units at x = 400 (below the 24-unit acceptance check) and at 8 by 27.7. Additions outside the PRD: a Menu button in the HUD, Play on the menu picks the first unlocked challenge with no stars, a red left-edge flash when a target reaches the line.

## v0.2 (unreleased)
- Built by Sonnet (review pending): review fixes and the v0.2 delta together. Review fixes: Menu button moved top-right beside the score, combo pips and multiplier moved to the ground band (out of the range-finder band, gunMinY unchanged); `pickY` now builds a legal interval and, if the band cannot allow `minDy`, takes the farthest point (Accuracy 3 places its low and high targets so every change is at least 70, Speed 3 uses minDy 50 because its band is 106 tall); the card ignores taps for 0.4 s; fire on touch down on the right half (hold fires only for auto guns); space through `onKey` (key repeat fires at the gun's rate); portrait shows a "Rotate your phone" banner over the ground band and stays playable.
- v0.2: gun profiles as data (Service pistol, Carbine, chosen on the menu and saved), accuracy sets the range finder length with exact shots, carbine fires on hold; barrel sway from gun movement (swayPerSpeed, swayMax, swayRecovery, measured over a 0.05 s window), added to the kick and shown on the range finder; weave, dodge, horde, skeet and boss behaviours; Accuracy 4, Speed 3 with weave, Speed 4, Skeet 1 and 2, Boss 1; four ladders on the menu; presets Fair, Twitchy, Lazy and four sliders (weaveAmp, skeetSpeed, carbine accuracy, swayPerSpeed). Save version 2, `migrate` keeps existing bests and adds the gun choice.
- Choices where the PRD is silent: a dodged shot is neutral (spends ammo, keeps the combo); dodge flickers while armed and for `dodgeWarn` 0.4 s before it re-arms; a skeet or horde target that is not shot is a miss and resets the combo; magSize and reloadSeconds are carried in the profile but unused (Accuracy keeps its ammo, the other ladders are unlimited); the old kickPerShot, kickRecovery, dragGain and approachSpeed experiments are dropped because the tune panel fits four sliders in landscape (kick is per gun now); the old kick, drag and approach experiments were dropped from the tune panel.
- Fire-rate conflict, resolved in the PRD: with the first build's pistol fireRate 4 (0.25 s between taps) equal to its kick recovery time (8 degrees at 32 per second) the kick could never stack, so the v0.1 "second quick shot lands 24 units higher" check could not pass. The PRD and code are now pistol fireRate 9 and carbine kickRecovery 24 (rule: a gun's fire interval must be shorter than kickPerShot / kickRecovery).
- Star thresholds by the v0.1 rule from scripted pistol runs (perfect scores): Accuracy 4 2950, Speed 4 3630, Skeet 1 2950, Skeet 2 3750, Boss 1 3750; existing challenges unchanged.
- Engine change (ADR-0014, done by the orchestrator): the tune panel restores only declared keys (sliders plus preset keys, so Twitchy and Lazy now persist) and prunes stale saved keys; Reset restores every declared key. This corrects the earlier note that preset values did not persist.
- Review round 2 (Sonnet): a hit on an inactive boss part is now neutral (no points, no damage, no combo change, not a hit; the shot is spent), so it cannot be farmed; saveVersion 3 and `migrate` prunes saved tune values to the declared keys; the horde spacing and jitters, skeet pair split and offset, launch spread and core x moved from `build` into the challenge data; a horde breaching together plays the miss sound and buzz once per 0.3 s; `gunMinY` is 60 (PRD v0.1 amended) so the Menu button never covers the range finder; the rotate hint is drawn in the letterbox below the field; the tap gate allows one step of slack, so a tap at the nominal interval is not dropped (shortest gap now 0.1083 s, 9.23 per second for the pistol; held auto fire keeps the exact rate).

## v0.3 (unreleased)
- Art layer built by Sonnet (review pending): procedural, no image assets, `game.js` only. Four gun silhouettes (service pistol, carbine, shotgun, marksman rifle) turn whole about the grip with the kick and sway, with a muzzle flash sprite and a capped ejected-casing arc; the range finder is dots scaled and faded with distance, orange near the muzzle, with a soft end cap when the gun's accuracy stops it short; paper range targets (still cards on posts, approach cards on a trolley line, hordes as small cards), clays (cyan rim, paper disc), bolted boss plates with a hp tray and a lit active plate (cyan), a glowing core; a range backdrop (horizon, distant wall with lane markers, ground band with brass-casing combo pips); menu gun tiles draw each silhouette (locked ones dimmed with a padlock and threshold), missions and card restyled. Every colour and effect number is in `TUNING.art` (the old top-level colour keys moved there; `TUNING.bg` is set from the palette); three text sizes. Menu gun tiles are 56 px tall (were 48) so the silhouettes fit. No sim, scoring, timing, save or tune change (30 and 120 fps runs identical to the previous build).
- Progression layer built by Sonnet (review pending): four guns unlocked by points, nine badges in three tiers, a missions screen, a gauntlet, handling presets. `game.js` only.
- Unlocks (points from the v0.1 rule, `TUNING.unlockPoints`): Service pistol 0, Carbine 60, Shotgun 150, Marksman rifle 300. The menu shows locked guns with their threshold, a bar and the next gun. A saved gun that is not unlocked plays as the pistol. Shotgun: five pellets in a fixed fan of `shotSpread` 10 degrees centred on the barrel (offsets -5, -2.5, 0, 2.5, 5), each pellet flies its own line and damages separately; scoring is amended below (review fixes). Rifle: damage 3. Both are semi-auto.
- Badges (data in `BADGES`; earned at the end of a run, once each): Bronze Marksman I (three stars Accuracy 1), Quick Draw I (Speed 1), Clay I (Skeet 1); Silver Steady (Accuracy 4 three stars with the carbine), Storm (Speed 4 three stars), Double (both clays of one Skeet 2 pair hit in one run); Gold Boss Killer (Boss 1 three stars with each of the four guns), Gauntlet (Accuracy 2, Speed 2, Skeet 2 and Boss 1 in a row from the missions screen, two stars or better each), Legend (three stars on every challenge). The card pops the badge; the menu shows the count.
- Save shape (version 4, well under a kilobyte or two): `best { id: { score, stars } }`, `gun`, `badges { badgeId: 1 }`, `bossGuns { gunId: 1 }` (guns that have three-starred Boss 1), `__tune`, `__muted`. Migration from 3: adds `badges` and `bossGuns`, awards the star-only badges the existing bests already earn (Marksman I, Quick Draw I, Clay I, Storm, Legend), prunes `__tune` to the declared keys; bests, gun and everything else are kept.
- Handling experiment replaces the v0.2 set: presets Steady (kick 5, sway 0.01), Standard (8, 0.02), Wild (12, 0.04) and sliders for both. Kick is per gun, so the key is `guns.pistol.kickPerShot`: it tunes the pistol; sway is shared. Declared keys are the two sliders, which are also the preset keys (ADR-0014).
- Decisions where the PRD is open: Double is read as "both clays of one Skeet 2 pair" (a double); the gauntlet is a chain started from the missions screen (Accuracy 2, Speed 2, Skeet 2, Boss 1, current gun, each Next after two stars, tapping Menu or failing a stage ends it) and needs those four unlocked; Boss Killer needs a three-star Boss 1 with all four guns, and because the shotgun and rifle finish Boss 1 in a handful of scoring shots (pistol thresholds 1130/2060/3190 are out of reach) they carry their own Boss 1 thresholds from their own maxima (see review fixes; pistol and carbine keep the pistol thresholds; nothing else is per gun); the shotgun's fire interval (1 / 2.3 = 0.435 s) is shorter than its kick recovery (14 / 30 = 0.467 s), so the v0.2 rule for guns holds and a second quick shot carries about 1 degree of kick (the rifle, 0.67 s against 0.8 s, stacks too; an earlier version of this line said the shotgun's interval was longer, which was wrong); `magSize` and `reloadSeconds` stay unused.
- Naked run holds: every challenge and every star is reachable with the pistol (all thresholds are pistol-derived); every badge except Steady and Boss Killer is earnable with it too. Steady needs the carbine (60 points) and Boss Killer needs all four guns, as the PRD states.
- Review fixes (Sonnet, on the amended PRD v0.3 section A; `game.js` only, plus the `sw.js` cache version):
  - Stars are monotonic: `endRun` saves `stars: max(saved, this run)` and the best score separately, so a higher score on a gun with easier thresholds never lowers stars or re-locks a gun. The card's best line now reads "Best 1200 · 3 stars saved" so it agrees with the save; the star row is still this run's.
  - Shotgun scoring: only the centre pellet scores zone points on ring targets; every pellet damages boss parts separately; on horde targets every member any pellet hits scores outer points (times the combo multiplier of the shot). Shot-level decisions where the PRD is silent: the combo advances once per shot however many members it hits, `hits` counts members, one pop shows the shot's total at the members' mean position, and a shot whose centre pellet misses but whose outer pellets damage a boss part is a miss for the combo (damage still lands, no points). Pistol, carbine and rifle results are unchanged (132 scripted runs identical to the previous build).
  - Boss 1 stars per gun are 30/55/85 percent of each gun's true maximum, found by searching every aim error, streak and pellet count and replaying the plan in the sim: pistol and carbine 3750 (1130/2060/3190, unchanged), rifle 1000 (300/550/850, unchanged), shotgun 1350 (410/740/1150; the earlier 300/550/850 came from a five-shot run). The shotgun's 1350 needs six scoring shots: the core takes three, so the centre pellet must sit in the bullseye off-centre enough that only one outer pellet lands on the core; a plain centred run tops out at 1000 (two stars).
  - Speed 4 with the shotgun and per-member scoring: perfect run 3630 (the same as the pistol's, which is the ceiling: 48 members, one shot each for the first six to build the combo); a plain sweep that hits five members every other shot scores 3150, already three stars against the pistol thresholds 1090/2000/3090. Left open for the designer: per-gun Speed 4 thresholds.
  - Clays are drawn in the goal colour (cyan rim, paper disc); the red dot is gone from bullseyes (red is danger only); the selected boss plate is lit in cyan; the lane boards on the wall are grey.
  - The badge pop scales the transform (from 78 percent of its size, faded in), never the font size; text is 14 px or more throughout. Hints and unearned badge conditions use `textDim` (was `textFaint`, 3.1 to 3.7:1).
  - The landscape menu column slides between 361 and 336 px tall by the height the viewport leaves above the bottom inset (all buttons stay 44 px or more), so it fits 640x360 and 812x375 with a 21 px inset.
  - A tap dropped inside the fire interval plays a faint click (volume 0.12) and a small faint muzzle flicker; timing and scoring are untouched (held auto fire does not cue).
  - Again after a passed Gauntlet stage replays that stage inside the chain (the card says "Gauntlet stage passed"); after a failed or final stage it starts a plain run. The dim Gauntlet button shows a padlock, and tapping it (or, in portrait, a caption below it) names the levels that still need a star.
