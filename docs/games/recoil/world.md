# World: Recoil

## The drive

- **What you do:** shoot props on a movie backlot's sets, take after take.
- **Why you do it:** ambition. You want to be the star.
- **For whom:** yourself: your name on the poster.
- **How it ends:** Action Star, and a wall of six premiere posters.

## Setting

Backlot 88, an invented studio that makes action movies, in a neon 1988. Pink and teal set lights, VHS lines, plywood flats (`style.md`).

## Places and things

- **Backlot 88:** the studio.
- **The sets:** Accuracy, Speed, Skeet, Boss, Zombies, Endless; each set, finished, is a film with a poster.
- **The Prop Room:** Gus's counter, where guns and skins are bought.
- **The poster wall:** one slot per set, "Coming soon" until released.

## Rules for this world

- Workplace and movie-set comedy. No line needs knowledge of a real film, actor or decade.
- Nobody is hurt: targets are props, zombies are extras in make-up.
- Every name is original.
