# Concept: Recoil

- **Slug:** recoil
- **Pattern:** P10 Spot and time, with an aim-under-recoil skill axis; L5 tiered progression (guns unlocked with challenge points)
- **Date:** 2026-09-28 (rewritten from the designer's synthesis reaction; the first draft had recoil as locomotion, which was wrong)
- **Status:** concept, gate passed

## One sentence

Move the gun up and down with one thumb, fire with the other, and fight the barrel, which kicks up with every shot, to land bullseyes on targets that come at you.

## The first ten seconds

Landscape. The gun sits at the left edge, a dotted range finder runs from its barrel across the field. A ring target appears on the right, three concentric zones. The player drags the left thumb down a little: the gun and the range finder drop onto the target. They tap with the right thumb: the shot is instant, the ring flashes "50", and the range finder jumps up as the barrel kicks. They tap again straight away and the shot flies over the target. They watch the range finder settle back down, tap, and hit the bullseye: "100".

## The fantasy

A marksman who knows their gun. Mastery looks like a rhythm of shots timed to the recoil, each one landing centre.

## Three tips

1. Wait for the barrel to settle; the range finder shows you when.
2. Or don't wait: nudge the gun down as you fire to cancel the kick.
3. Bullseyes are worth five times the outer ring; one calm shot beats three rushed ones.

## Perfect versus sloppy

An approaching target. The sloppy play mashes: the first shot clips the outer ring, the next two sail over it as the barrel climbs, and the target reaches the gun. The perfect play fires once, drops the gun a hair, fires into the bullseye on the recovery, and the target bursts with a "100" and a combo pip. The game rewards the perfect play with rings scored by precision, a multiplier for consecutive hits, and challenge stars that turn into unlock points.

## What it borrows

The Flash recoil game: unlockable guns with different characteristics, shooting targets, moving the gun vertically, attachments. What was good: the barrel fighting you, and guns that felt different in the hand.

## Guessed kernel

Verb: drag to move, tap to fire, manage the kick. Skill axis: timing and compensation against a deterministic recoil, plus precision on ringed targets. Content: two challenge ladders, Accuracy and Speed, three levels each in v0.1. A challenge lasts 20 to 40 seconds.
