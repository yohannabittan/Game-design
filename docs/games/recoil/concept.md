# Concept: Recoil

- **Slug:** recoil
- **Pattern:** P3 Recoil locomotion, later L1 run structure and guns as unlocks that change play
- **Date:** 2026-09-28
- **Status:** concept, gate passed; synthesis sent to the designer, reaction pending

## One sentence

You are a floating gun; every shot shoves you the other way, so you move by shooting; hit targets before they vanish and don't run dry.

## The first ten seconds

A dark arena walled on all sides. A small gun-shaped body hangs near the bottom, drifting down under gravity. A target ring appears up and to the right. The player touches the screen there: the gun turns to the touch, fires, the target bursts, and the gun kicks down and left. It sinks. The player touches below the gun; it fires downward and hops up. They understand.

## The fantasy

A trick-shot artist who never lands. Mastery looks like hanging in the air on a rhythm of shots while every bullet finds a target.

## Three tips

1. Shoot down to stay up. Short bursts, not sprays.
2. To reach a target, shoot away from it: recoil carries you in.
3. Watch the magazine. Hovering spends the same bullets as hunting.

## Perfect versus sloppy

Same round. The sloppy play sprays at targets, drifts into walls, runs dry mid-air, drops to the floor during the reload, and scores singles. The perfect play hovers on a two-shot rhythm, brakes into each target with one shot, reloads on the floor between waves, and strings a combo that multiplies every hit. The game gives the combo a rising pitch and a multiplier on screen; the floor touch resets it.

## What it borrows

The Flash recoil game: unlockable guns with different characteristics, shooting targets, fighting other gunhands, vertical movement, attachments. What was good: a single input doing two jobs, and guns that changed how you moved as much as how you shot.

## Guessed kernel

Verb: tap or hold to shoot toward the touch. Skill axis: momentum control through fire. Content: one arena, three target types, a 45-second round. Ends at the timer.
