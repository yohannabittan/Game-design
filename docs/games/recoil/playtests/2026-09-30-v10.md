## Decision input

- Evolve. Findings and answers:
  - Stars and scores too hard: PRD v0.5 A (bars from noisy bots, presets Pro/Skilled/Casual).
  - Accuracy targets not scoring or vanishing: PRD v0.5 B, investigation in progress.
  - Guns should show stats; skins uneven: PRD v0.5 C.
  - Missing umph: PRD v0.5 D (visible multiplier, call-outs, slow-motion, ticker).
  - Wrong tool was fun: PRD v0.5 E.
  - Menu boring: PRD v0.5 F.
  - Zombie mode as described (legs, body, brain; fence ends the run): PRD v0.5 G.
  - More guns: PRD v0.5 H.
  - Delighter badges (six hits in 0.5 s via recoil): PRD v0.5 J.

## Second session (v11, star bars live)

- The marksman rifle on a boss seems unable to reach three stars "due to damage". Diagnosis: a boss best is saved without its gun and the menu reads it against the pistol's bars (rifle maximum 2550 on Boss 2 against a pistol three-star bar of 7790). Answer: PRD v0.5 K (bests and stars per gun).
- The level selector still cannot say what was done with which gun, which the badges depend on; the designer proposes unlocking levels per gun as a distinct challenge. Answer: PRD v0.5 K (ladders unlock per gun, points as the sum of all guns' stars, gun unlocks by badges).

## Third session (v15, zombies)

- Concept good; brains at one height keep it too easy; wants zombie types, faster, speed and health climbing over time; hitting anything but the brain should lose the multiplier. Answer: PRD v0.5 L.
- Guns need to reload; planning ahead is the difficulty; a button for early reload. Answer: PRD v0.5 M.
