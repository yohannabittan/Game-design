# Ink step: the first minute (locked 2026-11-14)

**From:** the first-minute look (`docs/first-minute/2026-11-14-golf-ink-recoil.md`, "Ink") and the designer's go on its best fix. Principles 18 and 19.

**What a newcomer meets today:** the first scribble is ruined in about 3 seconds. Nothing shows that the needle sits ahead of the finger, or what a slip is, and the needle only appears after the first touch. A time-out at 68% with no slips shows the same sad client and the same line as a ruined piece.

**The goal:** a newcomer knows, before their first stroke, where the needle is and what staying inside means, by seeing and doing it. And when a try ends, they know why and what a star needed.

**What done looks like:**
- **On a fresh save's first piece,** the needle is on screen before the first touch, and a ghost drag shows one stroke along the line: the finger behind, the needle leading. At most eight words, if any ("Drag to ink. The needle leads.").
- **The first slip ever** is loud: a short word by the red X ("OUT") and the three slip marks pulse once.
- **Every ending says its reason and its target** in one line: "Time ran out at 68%. 85% for a star." or "Three slips. Stay inside the line." The numbers are the real ones.
- The lesson shows once (a save key read with a default), and never again on a veteran save.

**Out of bounds:** no new modes, no change to scoring or to the needle's offset, no engine change.

**The designer will judge it** by the before-and-after GIF of a newcomer's first 20 seconds.
