# World: Ink

## The drive

- **What you do:** ink stencils onto skin against the clock, without slipping.
- **Why you do it:** care and trust. A tattoo lasts a lifetime.
- **For whom:** the client in the chair, who wears your work forever.
- **How it ends:** Master, your own shop, and a portfolio of people wearing your work.

## Setting

The Lucky Needle, a warm little tattoo parlour on a busy street. Wood, brass, lamplight (`style.md`).

## Rules for this world

- Clients are adults who chose this; the tone is warm, never edgy.
- A ruined piece makes a client sad, never angry at you; the next one trusts you again.
