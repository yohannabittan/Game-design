# Playtest: Ink v0.3 needle inertia presets (cache ink-v9)

- **Date:** 2026-09-29
- **Context:** phone, the four presets compared on the TUNE tab.

## Decision

- **Flowy wins.** "The flowy dynamic needle is the best." Its values (growRate 14, shrinkRate 30, floorScale 0.8) become the defaults; presets and needle sliders are removed per the v0.3 decision rule.
- "We're at a really good place with this game."

## Finding

1. **The last few percent are hard to find, not hard to fill.** Above about 95 percent, the remaining gaps are slivers at the edges and the player scans for them while the timer runs. Proposal from the designer: after 90 or 95 percent, highlight what is missing, but only gaps that are at least about a tenth of what is left, so dust is not highlighted. Decision: v0.4 gap hint, threshold and share on the tune panel.
