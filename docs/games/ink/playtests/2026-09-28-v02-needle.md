## Findings

1. **Raw sliders are hard to reason about.** Seven knobs with no picture of the result. Decision: experiments ship as named presets (three or four per experiment) with the sliders underneath for fine tuning. Engine change.
2. **Needle width needs inertia.** The size of the ink dot is critical. Today it follows speed instantly, which reads as streaky and spray-like. Proposal: width grows toward its slow size at a limited rate (so holding still takes a moment to get wide), shrinks toward its fast size at a moderate rate (so a quick move does not collapse the line), and never drops below a floor. Fluid, flowy. Decision: Ink v0.3 as an experiment with presets.
3. **Slips are judged at the needle centre**, so a wider needle makes it easier not to spill. Noted; the designer thinks that may be fine as is. Left unchanged.

## Decision input

- Evolve. v0.3 needle inertia, then the art layer.
