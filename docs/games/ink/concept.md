# Concept: Ink

- **Slug:** ink
- **Pattern:** P2 Trace and fill, in its fill form (colour inside the lines), with L2 collection later (the gallery)
- **Date:** 2026-09-28
- **Status:** concept, gate passed after a synthesis session with the designer

## One sentence

Hold the tattoo gun and fill the stencil before the timer runs out; the needle sits ahead of your finger so you can see the ink land; leave the line three times and the piece is ruined.

## The first ten seconds

A dark skin-toned field with a stencil outline of a circle in light blue. The player touches the screen and a small tattoo machine appears with its needle a thumb's length ahead of the finger. They move; black ink fills where the needle passes, inside the stencil only. A percentage climbs in the corner. They sweep the middle, slow down near the edge, and the needle crosses the line once: a small red mark and a buzz. They keep going. The timer ends at 83 percent. Three stars pop in. Next.

## The fantasy

A tattoo artist with a steady hand. Mastery looks like riding the line at speed without ever crossing it.

## Three tips

1. Fill the middle fast, then do the edges slow. The middle is free percentage; the edges are where the slips are.
2. Work along the edge, never across it. Moving parallel to the line keeps the needle inside.
3. Lift at corners. Lifting is free; dragging through a sharp corner is a slip.

## Perfect versus sloppy

Same shape, a star. The sloppy play sweeps everything at one speed, slips twice on the points, and ends at 78 percent with two red marks on the skin: three stars, a scarred piece. The perfect play floods the centre in the first third of the timer, then rides each point's edges from the inside at half speed, lifting at every tip, and ends at 99 percent clean: five stars, a "clean" flourish, the piece flashes onto the skin without marks.

## What it borrows

The old Flash tattoo game: fill the shape within a time, limited times you may go out, score gates the next one. What was good: a single number you could always improve, and the finished piece as the reward. It adds the tattoo gun offset, which turns the phone's finger-occlusion problem into the game's identity.

## Guessed kernel

Verb: hold and move. Skill axis: precision near edges under time pressure. Content: 10 stencils ordered by edge difficulty. A session is one stencil, 20 to 45 seconds. Ends at 100 percent, at the timer, or at the third slip.

## Gate

- [x] Three tips are written and are about technique, not memorising layouts
- [x] Perfect and sloppy plays get visibly different responses
- [x] A naked run is possible by design: there is no equipment in v0.1
- [x] It works in portrait with one thumb
