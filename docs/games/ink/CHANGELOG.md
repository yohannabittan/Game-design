# Ink changelog

## v0.1 (unreleased)
- Created from skeleton.
- Layer 1 (mechanic), built by Sonnet: hold-and-move needle with the constant screen-pixel offset, distance-sampled coverage grid and once-per-exit slips, circle stencil with a 30 s timer, HUD, card, menu, best percentage saved. Open questions: ink drawn as smooth strokes with the grid only for scoring (clipped to the stencil so the two agree), stencil 1 timer generous so five stars is reachable. The timer starts on the first touch down.
- Layer 1 review fixes, Sonnet: card shows Next on a pass or Again on a fail (never both) plus a Menu button, slips are also tested at the real needle position of each event, a lost pointer up no longer locks out inking, and the timer is now larger than the slip counter.
- Layer 2 progression, Sonnet: unlock by one star, best percentage, stars and clean per stencil (saveVersion 2 with migrate from v1), select grid of 10 tiles, total stars on the menu, Next goes to the next stencil and stencil 10's pass card shows Menu; ink cached on an offscreen layer; stencils 2 to 10 are placeholders pending the content shards; tools/sim-ink.mjs added.
- Layer 2 content (Sonnet shards, sequence review found two timer errors by the orchestrator, fixed): nine stencils authored by three parallel Sonnet shards against tools/sim-ink.mjs and merged by the orchestrator (ADR-0012); timers set as a multiple of the perfect-path time; every stencil reaches 99 percent clean by its intended path and a naive sweep is ruined on each.
- Layer 3 juice, built by Sonnet: touch settle pop, needle glint, body vibration, rate-limited needle ticks and tip specks, slip flash/pop/shake/haptic, percentage and timer pops, finish flash and outline burst, grey smear on the third slip, card slide with staggered star pops and the Clean sweep, buttons enabled only after the beat, menu tile pop for a star gain; every number in TUNING.juice, five of them on the TUNE tab. Coverage, slips, timer, stars and saves untouched (sim numbers identical). sw.js CACHE_VERSION not bumped (out of this layer's file scope): bump on deploy. Skeleton note: E.audio.play has no delay, so the delayed lose after the miss is scheduled in play.update.

## v0.2 (unreleased)
- Dynamic needle experiment (PRD v0.2), built by Sonnet: ink radius follows finger speed over the last speedWindow units (slow wide, fast thin, linear), drawn stroke width follows it, menu toggle "Needle: classic / dynamic" saved (default dynamic), bests kept per mode (saveVersion 3, migrate keeps v0.1 bests as classic), tuning names and tune sliders for the four needle values, sim-ink takes [x, y, speed] points plus --needle and --events. Classic reproduces the v0.1 sim numbers exactly. Finding: riding every edge at slowSpeed makes the intended path 25 to 71 percent slower on eight of ten stencils, so timers need re-deriving if that is the intended play (proposals in the report, timers not changed). sw.js CACHE_VERSION not bumped here.

## v0.3 (unreleased)
- Needle inertia experiment (PRD v0.3), built by Sonnet: the dynamic ink radius now moves toward its speed target at growRate (widening) and shrinkRate (narrowing), floored at needleR * floorScale, integrated over each path sample by segment time (frame-rate independent) and in fixed 1/120 s steps while the finger holds still; the drawn stroke swells and tapers with it; Crisp, Flowy, Heavy and Marker presets plus four sliders on the TUNE tab (juice sliders dropped for now), Flowy the default; sim-ink takes --preset and { "hold": seconds } path entries. Classic mode unchanged. Finding: Flowy (floor 0.8) lets a fast sweep on the circle reach 84 to 85 percent, right at the PRD limit; floorScale 0.75 gives 72 to 73. sw.js CACHE_VERSION not bumped here.

## v0.4 (unreleased)
- Gap hint and needle cleanup, built by Sonnet (PRD v0.4): Flowy dynamic needle is the only needle (classic mode, the classic/dynamic toggle, needle presets and needle sliders removed; saveVersion 4 keeps dynamic bests, stars, clean and unlocks, drops classic bests and stale saved tune values); from hintPercent the unfilled cells are clustered (4-neighbour, recomputed every hintEvery s) and every cluster holding at least hintShare of what is left glows in stencil blue under the ink and outline; TUNE tab is now the gap hint only (Late, Standard, Early, Off presets, two sliders); sim-ink drops --needle and --preset. Simulator numbers unchanged on all ten dynamic paths. sw.js CACHE_VERSION not bumped here.
- Layer 5 art (tattoo flash), built by Sonnet: all procedural, every colour and drawing number in TUNING.art (old colour keys removed, `bg` now comes from the palette); a skin field (prebuilt grain, pores, vignette) with a soft-shaded body part behind each stencil (new `body` field per stencil: forearm, shoulder, calf or back), a wobbly per-stencil-seeded stencil-blue outline with a faint double edge, near-black ink with a blue sheen pattern and a soft bleed halo, a skin-redness halo on the card, card-only story decorations (gem facets from 95 percent to 100, a ribbon across the heart, a swirl in the star; new `story` field), a pen-style machine with knurled grip, steel nozzle, visible needle tip and a trailing cable, a flash-sheet menu on a paper board with pinned cards showing each design and a brush-stroke INK title, a HUD with the percentage as hero and the timer and slips on plates, and a result card with the finished piece large on skin above a paper card with ink stars and a Clean stamp. No font or image assets (no OFL brush font found). Coverage, slips, timers, polygons, scoring, saves and the hint are untouched (simulator numbers identical). sw.js CACHE_VERSION was not bumped by this layer (the orchestrator bumped it to ink-v11 with the v0.5 merge and regenerated the icons for the new palette).

## v0.5 (unreleased)
- Content: five stencils (dagger, anchor, swallow, rose, skull boss) authored by two parallel Sonnet shards against tools/sim-ink.mjs and merged by the orchestrator; fifteen stencils. The first merge ordered by perfect-path time and the review found that inverts edge difficulty, so the final order is Circle, Diamond, Heart, Star, Bolt, Crescent (boss), Halo, Clover, Dagger, Anchor, Rose, Swallow, Snake (boss), Key, Skull (final boss) per PRD v0.5 section A, bosses at 6, 13 and 15; the skull's timer is 39 s (1.35x its perfect path, new `timerMultSkull`).
- Review fixes to the merge and the art, Sonnet: saveVersion 5 with a migration that remaps best, stars, clean and unlocked from the ten-stencil layout ([0,1,2,3,5,4,6,7,13,12]) or the interim fifteen-stencil layout of cache v11 ([0,1,2,3,8,5,9,4,6,11,10,7,13,12,14], detected by any key or unlock at 10 or above); no text under 14 px (BOSS badge widened, Clean is a sticker over the preview, BOSS also shows on locked boss cards); the menu is laid out from the available height (title shrinks, then tiles, title clears the engine's TUNE tab) and fits 360x640, 375x667 and 390x844 with 44 px targets; ink strokes are drawn as one path per run of similar width (skull inking at 4x throttle roughly halved) and the menu prebuilds the play textures one per frame; menu pins are brass and the finished-piece halo is a warm skin tint (red is only for slips); skin darkened and its highlight lowered, the outline lighter with a heavier dark keyline (outline contrast well over 3:1); the faint second edge only moves inward (never past the slip tolerance); shoulder, calf and forearm silhouettes widened so no stencil edge sits on the drape; hintAlphaMin 0.25. Stale ten-stencil text fixed in game.js, README and the simulator's timer rows. sw.js CACHE_VERSION needs another bump on deploy.
- Missions, daily stencil and timer presets (PRD v0.5 sections B, C, D), built by Sonnet: saveVersion 6 adds `cs` (best stars of a zero-slip pass per stencil), `badges` ({ id: true }) and `daily` ({ 'YYYY-MM-DD': { idx, vi, first, best } }, last 7 days); migrate keeps everything, sets `cs` from any stencil with a clean pass and earns badges from the stars and clean passes already saved. Ten badges as data (tier, name, text, rule: a stat on any stencil, one stencil by name, or a range) in three tiers, a Missions screen from the menu (scrolls, shows progress on range badges), a badge ticket over the card when one is earned. Daily strip on the menu: chosen once per UTC day with makeRng(hashString(date)) from the unlocked stencils, turned by one of three variants (Mirror, Flipped, Turned, about its own centre, so its perfect path is unchanged), timer 1.4x the perfect path (never longer than its own), the first attempt is the daily score and the day's best is shown, no stars, unlocks or badges from it, replays allowed. The menu now has a Missions button beside the star count, the daily strip, and Play with a speaker toggle. TUNE tab: Relaxed (2.6x), Standard, Tight (1.6x; the skull keeps 1.35x) presets and a global timer multiplier slider; the hint's four presets became its two sliders (101 is off). Timers are computed from each stencil's tier, so `tools/sim-ink.mjs --preset` and `--timer-from-path` follow the active multipliers. Simulator numbers unchanged on all ten dynamic paths and the skull path. sw.js needs a CACHE_VERSION bump on deploy.
