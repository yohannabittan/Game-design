# Ink changelog

## v0.1 (unreleased)
- Created from skeleton.
- Layer 1 (mechanic), built by Sonnet: hold-and-move needle with the constant screen-pixel offset, distance-sampled coverage grid and once-per-exit slips, circle stencil with a 30 s timer, HUD, card, menu, best percentage saved. Open questions: ink drawn as smooth strokes with the grid only for scoring (clipped to the stencil so the two agree), stencil 1 timer generous so five stars is reachable. The timer starts on the first touch down.
- Layer 1 review fixes, Sonnet: card shows Next on a pass or Again on a fail (never both) plus a Menu button, slips are also tested at the real needle position of each event, a lost pointer up no longer locks out inking, and the timer is now larger than the slip counter.
- Layer 2 progression, Sonnet: unlock by one star, best percentage, stars and clean per stencil (saveVersion 2 with migrate from v1), select grid of 10 tiles, total stars on the menu, Next goes to the next stencil and stencil 10's pass card shows Menu; ink cached on an offscreen layer; stencils 2 to 10 are placeholders pending the content shards; tools/sim-ink.mjs added.
- Layer 2 content (Sonnet shards, sequence review found two timer errors by the orchestrator, fixed): nine stencils authored by three parallel Sonnet shards against tools/sim-ink.mjs and merged by the orchestrator (ADR-0012); timers set as a multiple of the perfect-path time; every stencil reaches 99 percent clean by its intended path and a naive sweep is ruined on each.
- Layer 3 juice, built by Sonnet: touch settle pop, needle glint, body vibration, rate-limited needle ticks and tip specks, slip flash/pop/shake/haptic, percentage and timer pops, finish flash and outline burst, grey smear on the third slip, card slide with staggered star pops and the Clean sweep, buttons enabled only after the beat, menu tile pop for a star gain; every number in TUNING.juice, five of them on the TUNE tab. Coverage, slips, timer, stars and saves untouched (sim numbers identical). sw.js CACHE_VERSION not bumped (out of this layer's file scope): bump on deploy. Skeleton note: E.audio.play has no delay, so the delayed lose after the miss is scheduled in play.update.

## v0.2 (unreleased)
- Dynamic needle experiment (PRD v0.2), built by Sonnet: ink radius follows finger speed over the last speedWindow units (slow wide, fast thin, linear), drawn stroke width follows it, menu toggle "Needle: classic / dynamic" saved (default dynamic), bests kept per mode (saveVersion 3, migrate keeps v0.1 bests as classic), tuning names and tune sliders for the four needle values, sim-ink takes [x, y, speed] points plus --needle and --events. Classic reproduces the v0.1 sim numbers exactly. Finding: riding every edge at slowSpeed makes the intended path 25 to 71 percent slower on eight of ten stencils, so timers need re-deriving if that is the intended play (proposals in the report, timers not changed). sw.js CACHE_VERSION not bumped here.

## v0.3 (unreleased)
- Needle inertia experiment (PRD v0.3), built by Sonnet: the dynamic ink radius now moves toward its speed target at growRate (widening) and shrinkRate (narrowing), floored at needleR * floorScale, integrated over each path sample by segment time (frame-rate independent) and in fixed 1/120 s steps while the finger holds still; the drawn stroke swells and tapers with it; Crisp, Flowy, Heavy and Marker presets plus four sliders on the TUNE tab (juice sliders dropped for now), Flowy the default; sim-ink takes --preset and { "hold": seconds } path entries. Classic mode unchanged. Finding: Flowy (floor 0.8) lets a fast sweep on the circle reach 84 to 85 percent, right at the PRD limit; floorScale 0.75 gives 72 to 73. sw.js CACHE_VERSION not bumped here.

## v0.4 (unreleased)
- Gap hint and needle cleanup, built by Sonnet (PRD v0.4): Flowy dynamic needle is the only needle (classic mode, the classic/dynamic toggle, needle presets and needle sliders removed; saveVersion 4 keeps dynamic bests, stars, clean and unlocks, drops classic bests and stale saved tune values); from hintPercent the unfilled cells are clustered (4-neighbour, recomputed every hintEvery s) and every cluster holding at least hintShare of what is left glows in stencil blue under the ink and outline; TUNE tab is now the gap hint only (Late, Standard, Early, Off presets, two sliders); sim-ink drops --needle and --preset. Simulator numbers unchanged on all ten dynamic paths. sw.js CACHE_VERSION not bumped here.
- Layer 5 art (tattoo flash), built by Sonnet: all procedural, every colour and drawing number in TUNING.art (old colour keys removed, `bg` now comes from the palette); a skin field (prebuilt grain, pores, vignette) with a soft-shaded body part behind each stencil (new `body` field per stencil: forearm, shoulder, calf or back), a wobbly per-stencil-seeded stencil-blue outline with a faint double edge, near-black ink with a blue sheen pattern and a soft bleed halo, a skin-redness halo on the card, card-only story decorations (gem facets from 95 percent to 100, a ribbon across the heart, a swirl in the star; new `story` field), a pen-style machine with knurled grip, steel nozzle, visible needle tip and a trailing cable, a flash-sheet menu on a paper board with pinned cards showing each design and a brush-stroke INK title, a HUD with the percentage as hero and the timer and slips on plates, and a result card with the finished piece large on skin above a paper card with ink stars and a Clean stamp. No font or image assets (no OFL brush font found). Coverage, slips, timers, polygons, scoring, saves and the hint are untouched (simulator numbers identical). sw.js CACHE_VERSION was not bumped by this layer (the orchestrator bumped it to ink-v11 with the v0.5 merge and regenerated the icons for the new palette). Review: did not pass first time (four blocking findings on the art and the fifteen-stencil merge; fixed under v0.5 below).

- Missions, daily stencil and timer presets (PRD v0.5 sections B, C, D), built by Sonnet: saveVersion 6 adds `cs` (best stars of a zero-slip pass per stencil), `badges` ({ id: true }) and `daily` ({ 'YYYY-MM-DD': { idx, vi, first, best } }, last 7 days); migrate keeps everything, sets `cs` from any stencil with a clean pass and earns badges from the stars and clean passes already saved. The menu now has a Missions button beside the star count, the daily strip, and Play with a speaker toggle. TUNE tab: Relaxed (2.6x), Standard, Tight (1.6x; the skull keeps 1.35x) presets and a global timer multiplier slider; the hint's four presets became its two sliders (101 is off). ## v0.6 (unreleased)
- Timer, slip feedback, flat body parts and stencils in parts (PRD v0.6 sections A to E), built by Sonnet (a build continued from a lost session's partial edits after the 13 unchanged paths were re-run and matched). Timer: a 44 px numeral in a ring (radius 38) that drains clockwise, centred under the safe inset (never under the thumb), cream, amber at a third of the time, pulsing in the last five seconds; percent moves left at 34 px; the slip plate stays right; a timer of 100 s or more uses the 20 px numeral. Slip: one flinch per slip (the piece jolts 3 units away from the needle and settles in 0.25 s, restarted and never stacked), one 2 px 80 ms screen kick, a low thud under the slip sound, a 20 ms buzz through `E.haptic` (50 ms on the ruin), the slip mark unchanged. Flat body parts (section C, option 2): one flat skin tone (`skin`, replacing the light, mid and dark gradient), one soft shade and one soft highlight per part, smooth silhouettes (straight sides or one long curve; the creases, the calf groove, the shoulder strap, the spine beads and the neck notch are gone), a softer rim with one clean edge line; the style sentence in style.md now says so; outline contrast is above the v12 figures on every stencil (median and 10th percentile, sampled: v12 medians 3.24 to 3.95 and p10 2.79 to 3.10, now 3.75 to 4.07 and 3.33 to 3.62). Parts: a stencil may have `parts`; a part is done at 99 percent of its cells and fills itself, the piece at every part; a touch down is a landing, clean inside a line or within `landTolerance` (2) of it, else a blot (a slip: red disc with three specks, the same flinch, three ruin); the card shows `Landings n / m clean` and grows 36 px for such stencils; badges Steady Landing (Artist: a pass with every landing clean) and Set Piece (Master: five stars on every stencil in parts, two for now); saveVersion 8 adds `land` ({ idx: { n, c, ok } }) with a migrate that changes nothing else. The Swallow is three parts (two wings and the body, slits 5 units wide) and the Skull seven (cranium and jaw, six abutting teeth cut from the jaw by a 5 unit slit); their paths were rewritten per part (lifting over the gaps), because the old paths with lifts inserted reached 82 and 25 percent (the radius restarts thin at each landing). Perfect times and timers: Swallow 17.47 to 16.42 s, timer 34 to 32; Skull 28.82 to 30.92 s, timer 39 to 42. The other thirteen paths give the same percent, slips and 99 percent moment. The simulator reads `parts` and lifts, prints landings and each part's fill, and times a piece in parts to its last part done.
