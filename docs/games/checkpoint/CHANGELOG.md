# Checkpoint changelog

## v0.1 (unreleased)
- Created from skeleton.
- Layer 1 (mechanic), built by Sonnet: grey-box belt for shift 1 (20 seeded bags, tap-to-flag with contraband winning overlaps, catch/clean/false-alarm/miss scoring with early factor and x1 to x4 streak, three strikes, end card with stars and retry, menu with mute), the full 30-item table and the ten-row shift table as data in `TUNING`, and `tools/sim-checkpoint.mjs` (packing, bot and fps-determinism proofs). Open questions resolved with the simplest option: the belt never pauses for a full bag; a false alarm leaves the bag on the belt and its contraband stays catchable. Also chosen: a caught bag keeps riding down (no tray yet); contraband unlocks by shift (3 shapes in shift 1, all 10 from shift 6); shift 1 opens with the PRD's shirt, phone, headphones and scissors bag, then a clean bag; every shift has exactly 10 clean bags of 20 (clean share 0.5); overlap is off in shifts 1 and 2 and on from shift 3. Review: passed (release gate, v4; v5 pre-release round checked by the orchestrator)
- Checkpoint v2 (layer 1 review amendments 1 to 8, then layer 2 content), built by Sonnet; the layer 1 review failed on the pre-miss pulse, so this build is not yet reviewed. Review fixes: no red tell on uncaught contraband, the miss cue is a ghost outline pulsing at the bottom edge for 1 s after the strike (harness: no red on screen before the first strike, and a pulse-waiting bot fails every shift from shift 1); early factor 3 down to 0.5 and streak x2 to x5 at 4/8/12/16 (fast clean over late one-strike about 3 to 3.8); `hitMargin` 12 and every item at least 20 units at its narrowest (hit shape 44) with toothbrush and pen thickened and keys drawn with a hollow ring; visibility checked by area and by outline perimeter at 60 percent (harness, finer grids than packing); phone/lighter and belt/hammer look-alikes; first bag fully in view at t=0; clean bag settles (green edge, +N) when its bottom edge reaches the belt end; bottom cues above the safe inset. Layer 2: all ten shifts playable, each unlocked by clearing the one before (save key `unlocked`), shift select on the menu with stars and best per shift and a RUSH tag on 5 and 10, Next shift on the card, per-shift `twoShare`, `overlapBias` and `confusableShare` columns tuned against a human-model bot (three stars 90 to 98 percent on shifts 1 to 3, about half on shift 10), teaching goal and intended solution per shift in the `TUNING` comment. `bagH` 260 and `bagGap` 64 (PRD 40) so shift 10 is 40 s long at the given belt speeds. Review: passed (release gate, v4; v5 pre-release round checked by the orchestrator)
- Checkpoint v3 (v2 review amendments 1 to 6, then layer 3 juice and layer 5 art), built by Sonnet. Amendments: save v2 (`migrate` derives `unlocked` from the highest starred shift plus one; `unlocked` is clamped to an integer 1 to 10 on read); the difficulty is a slope (belt 70 to 105 units/s with shift 10 back at 95, clutter, look-alike and pair columns re-tuned; harness runs the reviewer's four human models, average reader clears every shift at least 80 percent and three-stars shift 10 25 percent, novice clears shifts 1 to 6 at least 80 percent, good reader three-stars rush shifts 5 and 10 about 19 and 15 points below their neighbours); rush hour is bursts of three bags (gap 12 inside, 168 between, same average pitch) with a RUSH banner per burst and a louder hum; tangle bias (`tangleOpposite` 0.9: overlap partners come from the other tint, metal-on-metal overlaps 10 percent); toothbrush and belt are metal so every look-alike pair shares a tint; Retry reuses the seed. Juice (PRD section 10): catch ring snaps in with `hit` and 10 ms, "+N" pops, score pops, sparks in red and the item's tint (particle cap 140), big catches (900 points and up) shake, the bag slides into the tray and takes no more taps; clean pass has a glowing green edge and a two-note chime; false alarm has a yellow ring, bag flash, buzzer, 30 ms and a shake; a miss has the ghost pulse, a low buzz, 40 ms and a shake; a streak step pops the badge with a rising tone; CLEARED and SHIFT OVER stamps slam down with `win` and 20 ms or `lose`; the card counts up, pops the stars and shows its buttons after the beat; taps ripple at once and buttons shrink while pressed; the belt hum (short overlapping triangle beeps, the engine has no sustained oscillator) rises with the streak. Art (PRD section 11, anchor in `docs/games/checkpoint/style.md`): palette, three text sizes and line weights in `TUNING`, three-pass bloom outlines with additive overlaps, lit belt with scrolling slats and rollers, scanner hood with curtain strips the bags slide out from (`hoodH` 34 px, the first bag starts below it); render is item-neutral (harness: an unflagged contraband and harmless item of one tint draw with identical styles). Icons not regenerated. Likely first tuning: `bigCatch` and shake sizes, hum gain, `tray` speed, burst gap on rush shifts. Review: passed (release gate, v4; v5 pre-release round checked by the orchestrator)
- Checkpoint v4 (pre-release round: v3 review amendments 1 to 10), built by Sonnet. Frame budget: at 4x CPU throttle, DPR 3, shift 10 in headless software raster the median frame went from 67 to 100 ms (v3) to 16.7 ms and p95 from 117 to 133 ms to 33.4 ms (two vsyncs; not under 33 ms: 13 to 50 percent of frames still miss one vsync, the box was shared) by painting each item once into a sprite (bloom and all) and copying it, dropping the bag fill and belt slats, patterns and dashes (both slower), one-path belts and hood, culling under the hood, and a measured, adaptive bloom (share of slow frames in the first 1.5 s: 3 passes, 1, or none, fixed for the shift). No hitch entering a shift: the shift is built a few placements a frame behind the shift card (or the result card, for Retry) and play starts from ready objects (entering play 1 to 5 ms at 4x, was 840 to 1240). New today shift card (knife/scissors/gun, hammer/lighter, large liquid/batteries, fireworks, taser, box cutter, then a note) and a named miss ghost ("Missed: fireworks"). Tint prior kept in shifts 1 and 2, balanced from shift 3 (organic contraband is picked with weight, organic harmless less): P(contraband | organic) is 0.52 to 0.94 of P(contraband | metal) from shift 3. Core stroke is non-additive so amber stays amber and blue stays blue; a false alarm turns clearly yellow, a catch red. First-timer labels: "Streak x2" badge, "+230, early", "Clean +20", no seed on the card, "Clear shift N first" toast on a locked shift. Eyes closed: a false alarm is two 660 Hz blips and a 25-45-25 ms double pulse, a miss is one 330 Hz buzz and an 80 ms pulse (an octave apart, above the 58 to 218 Hz hum). Saves with malformed `shifts` read as empty; contrast checked in the harness (Next button text 5.4, RUSH label 7.0, locked RUSH 4.6). Icons regenerated (blue tile, C) and manifest colours set to the belt colour; `index.html` still carries the old theme-color meta. ADR-0016: ledger entries for the shift result (score, strikes, missed, false alarms, stars, catches, passes, time), retry, quit and shapes seen; `E.titleArea` set; release channel checked on a release-marked copy (save key `game:checkpoint.release`, EXPORT lists all four kinds; the game has no TUNE). Difficulty slope re-tuned for the tint change (shift 9 is now a breather: `confusableShare` 0.15; shift 10 0.75): average reader clears every shift at least 88 percent and three-stars shift 10 at 20 percent at 60 seeds (18 at 40, 24 at 100: the target of 20 holds, the small runs are noise), good reader three-stars rush shifts 5 and 10 about 12 and 13 points below their neighbours. Review: passed (release gate, v4; v5 pre-release round checked by the orchestrator)
