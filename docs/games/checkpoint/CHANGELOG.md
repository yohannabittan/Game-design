# Checkpoint changelog

## v0.1 (unreleased)
- Created from skeleton.
- Layer 1 (mechanic), built by Sonnet: grey-box belt for shift 1 (20 seeded bags, tap-to-flag with contraband winning overlaps, catch/clean/false-alarm/miss scoring with early factor and x1 to x4 streak, three strikes, end card with stars and retry, menu with mute), the full 30-item table and the ten-row shift table as data in `TUNING`, and `tools/sim-checkpoint.mjs` (packing, bot and fps-determinism proofs). Open questions resolved with the simplest option: the belt never pauses for a full bag; a false alarm leaves the bag on the belt and its contraband stays catchable. Also chosen: a caught bag keeps riding down (no tray yet); contraband unlocks by shift (3 shapes in shift 1, all 10 from shift 6); shift 1 opens with the PRD's shirt, phone, headphones and scissors bag, then a clean bag; every shift has exactly 10 clean bags of 20 (clean share 0.5); overlap is off in shifts 1 and 2 and on from shift 3. - Checkpoint v2 (layer 1 review amendments 1 to 8, then layer 2 content), built by Sonnet; the layer 1 review failed on the pre-miss pulse, so this build is not yet reviewed. Review fixes: no red tell on uncaught contraband, the miss cue is a ghost outline pulsing at the bottom edge for 1 s after the strike (harness: no red on screen before the first strike, and a pulse-waiting bot fails every shift from shift 1); early factor 3 down to 0.5 and streak x2 to x5 at 4/8/12/16 (fast clean over late one-strike about 3 to 3.8); `hitMargin` 12 and every item at least 20 units at its narrowest (hit shape 44) with toothbrush and pen thickened and keys drawn with a hollow ring; visibility checked by area and by outline perimeter at 60 percent (harness, finer grids than packing); phone/lighter and belt/hammer look-alikes; first bag fully in view at t=0; clean bag settles (green edge, +N) when its bottom edge reaches the belt end; bottom cues above the safe inset. Layer 2: all ten shifts playable, each unlocked by clearing the one before (save key `unlocked`), shift select on the menu with stars and best per shift and a RUSH tag on 5 and 10, Next shift on the card, per-shift `twoShare`, `overlapBias` and `confusableShare` columns tuned against a human-model bot (three stars 90 to 98 percent on shifts 1 to 3, about half on shift 10), teaching goal and intended solution per shift in the `TUNING` comment. `bagH` 260 and `bagGap` 64 (PRD 40) so shift 10 is 40 s long at the given belt speeds. Review: pending
- Checkpoint v3 (v2 review amendments 1 to 6, then layer 3 juice and layer 5 art), built by Sonnet. Amendments: save v2 (`migrate` derives `unlocked` from the highest starred shift plus one; `unlocked` is clamped to an integer 1 to 10 on read); the difficulty is a slope (belt 70 to 105 units/s with shift 10 back at 95, clutter, look-alike and pair columns re-tuned; harness runs the reviewer's four human models, average reader clears every shift at least 80 percent and three-stars shift 10 25 percent, novice clears shifts 1 to 6 at least 80 percent, good reader three-stars rush shifts 5 and 10 about 19 and 15 points below their neighbours); rush hour is bursts of three bags (gap 12 inside, 168 between, same average pitch) with a RUSH banner per burst and a louder hum; tangle bias (`tangleOpposite` 0.9: overlap partners come from the other tint, metal-on-metal overlaps 10 percent); toothbrush and belt are metal so every look-alike pair shares a tint; Retry reuses the seed. Juice (PRD section 10): catch ring snaps in with `hit` and 10 ms, "+N" pops, score pops, sparks in red and the item's tint (particle cap 140), big catches (900 points and up) shake, the bag slides into the tray and takes no more taps; clean pass has a glowing green edge and a two-note chime; false alarm has a yellow ring, bag flash, buzzer, 30 ms and a shake; a miss has the ghost pulse, a low buzz, 40 ms and a shake; a streak step pops the badge with a rising tone; CLEARED and SHIFT OVER stamps slam down with `win` and 20 ms or `lose`; the card counts up, pops the stars and shows its buttons after the beat; taps ripple at once and buttons shrink while pressed; the belt hum (short overlapping triangle beeps, the engine has no sustained oscillator) rises with the streak. Art (PRD section 11, anchor in `docs/games/checkpoint/style.md`): palette, three text sizes and line weights in `TUNING`, three-pass bloom outlines with additive overlaps, lit belt with scrolling slats and rollers, scanner hood with curtain strips the bags slide out from (`hoodH` 34 px, the first bag starts below it); render is item-neutral (harness: an unflagged contraband and harmless item of one tint draw with identical styles). Icons not regenerated. Likely first tuning: `bigCatch` and shake sizes, hum gain, `tray` speed, burst gap on rush shifts. Review: pending
