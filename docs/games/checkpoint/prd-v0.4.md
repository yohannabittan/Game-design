# Checkpoint PRD v0.4: Pat's Last Week

Status: locked 2026-10-12 by the designer ("Go"). Draft 1 was asked to "make the emotional momentum in the narrative a bit better", and draft 2 is this one.

**Where it comes from:** the arc pitched with Lumen and Sam, Backlot 88 and Rita's Swallow (protection, and passing the torch), in the Launch v0.6 style.

**What it builds on:** v0.3 was locked but never built. v0.4 keeps v0.3's machinery:
- a flight per day;
- the plane on the end card;
- the travellers' bubbles;
- the departures board;
- the fallbacks.

**What doesn't change:** rules, items, timings, scoring and ranks. The art has landed: `supervisor.png`, `supervisor-proud.png` and `plane.png` (Pack 10).

## What was wrong with draft 1

- **Days 1 to 7 were flat.** Pat gave tips, and nothing between her and you changed, so the turn on day 8 arrived cold.
- **Vic was comic relief with no stakes.** He had nothing to do with Pat or her flight.
- **You never acted in the story.** The ending happened *to* you; nothing you did on the belt was part of it.
- **There was no low point before the climax,** and no callback to tie the ending to the start.

## The arc, beat by beat

| Days | Beat | What changes, where you see it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cold open | **Setup.** Pat retires in ten days, the lane will be yours, and Vic has never got one past her in thirty years. | the lanyard |
| 1 to 3 | **Learning.** Pat teaches, dry and calm. | the first object on your desk (B) |
| 4 | **Warming.** Pat lets slip something personal: the grandkids, the knitting she's taking up. | a second object, and her lines start to joke |
| 5 | **The test.** Rush hour, and Pat is "in the back": you run the lane alone. The briefing card is just a sticky note in her writing. | if cleared, she comes back with `supervisor-proud`: "Didn't need me, did you?" |
| 6 to 7 | **Vic escalates.** He knows Pat is leaving, and he's playing to win before she goes. | his bubbles turn personal |
| 8 | **The turn.** Pat's flight home is Flight 10. And Vic has booked a ticket on it. | the turn scene (C) |
| 9 | **The low point.** Pat is off packing. Flight 9 is yours alone, and Vic does a dry run. The briefing is your first without her, and the end line is Pat on the phone. | the empty chair on the briefing |
| 10 | **The climax.** In one shift: Vic's masterpiece bag, and Pat's own bag on your belt. | two fixed bags (D) |
| End | **Resolution and callback.** The plane takes off, the radio says "Lane's yours", and next morning a new recruit hears Pat's first line from you. | the ending (E) |

## A. The cold open: three animated scenes

Played on the first launch, with Skip always visible, and replayable from Story. The scene style follows Launch A2: about 3 to 4 s each, portraits plus drawn props, squash-and-stretch tweens, and the text fading in underneath.

1. **The lane at dawn.** A plane on the stand through the window. Pat (`supervisor`) taps her clipboard. "Supervisor Pat Okoye has run this lane for thirty years."
2. **The lanyard.** She holds it out, and it swings to you. "'Ten days till I retire. Then this lane is yours.'"
3. **Vic.** At the back of the queue, a whistling man with a lumpy suitcase tips his hat to her. "'That's Vic. Thirty years, and he's never got one past me.'"

## C. The turn (the day 8 briefing, animated, once)

1. Pat sits on the edge of the desk and shows you a photo: two small drawn kids waving. "'Flight 10 is my flight home. They're waiting at the other end.'"
2. She turns the passenger list toward you, and one name is circled. "'And look who booked a seat.'" The list says VIC.
3. She stands. "'He wants his masterpiece on my plane. Don't let him.'"

One tap per beat, with Skip visible. Replayable from Story once seen.

## D. Vic's thread, and day 10's two bags

- **His bubble lines, when stopped:**

  | Days | Vic |
  | --- | --- |
  | 1 to 3 | "Just holiday things!", "Never seen it before!" |
  | 4 to 5 | "New trick, Officer!", "You're good. I'm better." |
  | 6 to 7 | "She's leaving. Then it's just you and me." |
  | 9 | "Just a practice run, Officer." |
  | 10 | "This one's my masterpiece." Caught: "Thirty years... and she trained you right." |

- **Vic's masterpiece.** A fixed bag in day 10's data: the game's hardest existing look-alike, from existing items only. Pat's day 7 and day 10 lines hint at it.
- **Pat's bag.** A fixed, clean bag later in day 10's queue, with her photo on the traveller tag. It carries the farewell gifts from the regulars, all existing harmless items:
  - Grandma Rose's spare knitting needles, for Pat's new hobby. They are the knife look-alike, so clearing them is a real read;
  - Mr Abernathy's toy, for the grandkids (not a snow globe, since a snow globe is contraband);
  - a book.

  Clear it and Pat smiles: "'Look at every bag. Even mine.'" Flagging it is a false alarm, as for any clean bag, and Pat laughs: "'Good. Never trust a supervisor.'" Only existing items and rules are used. The bubble is the only new thing.
- **Grandma Rose and Mr Abernathy** keep their v0.3 lines. On day 10 each adds one goodbye line for Pat when cleared: "Give the little ones a hug, Pat!" and "Safe travels, Supervisor!"

## E. The ending (day 10 cleared, the first time; animated, before the board)

1. Pat, now a passenger, hands you her mug (WORLD'S OKAYEST SUPERVISOR, which joins the desk). "'Not bad, Officer. Not bad at all.'"
2. The plane (`plane`) lifts off, and a tiny figure waves from a window. "Flight 10 landed. 240 passengers home safe."
3. The radio on the desk crackles. "'Lane's yours, Officer.'"
4. **Next morning:** a nervous new recruit at your lane, and your hand passes them the lanyard. "'First day. Breathe. Look at every bag.'" Then the departures board, as v0.3 E.

**After it,** the menu calls you "Supervisor" under the title, and the desk keeps Pat's things.

**If day 10 is a breach:** the plane stays on the stand. "Pat's flight didn't take off. The kids will wait a little longer." Smaller, Pat's voice: "'Again. I'm not going anywhere till you do.'"

## F. Art

No new art is needed. The kids' photo, the desk objects, the passenger list, the recruit and the waving figure are drawn by the game. `supervisor-proud` is used on day 5, day 9's 3-star line and the ending's first beat.

## G. Saves

`saveVersion` 5 adds:
- `openSeen` (as v0.3);
- `turnSeen`;
- `endSeen`;
- `desk`, derived from the cleared days, so it needs no storing (simplest option).

`migrate`:
- a played save marks `openSeen`;
- a save past day 8 marks `turnSeen`;
- a cleared day 10 does **not** mark `endSeen`, so a veteran sees the ending once, after their next cleared day 10.

## H. Out of scope

- new items, rules, ranks or days;
- voice;
- new art;
- any change to the X-ray, the lever, scoring or the detector.

## I. Acceptance

- Text counts per principle 11, and every line at most about 80 characters.
- Every story element skips in one tap and never delays a shift.
- Day 10's two fixed bags are in data, Vic's is catchable by its tell, and Pat's is clean.
- The desk shows exactly the cleared days' objects.
- A v4 save migrates as in G.
- The fallbacks draw while art is missing.
- `npm run smoke` passes.
- Frame time is no worse than checkpoint-v11.

**Rough cost:** one Sonnet build, about $9 to $12 and about an hour.
