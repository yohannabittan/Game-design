# Checkpoint step: the first minute (locked 2026-11-14)

**From:** the first-minute look (`docs/first-minute/2026-11-14-launch-checkpoint.md`, "Checkpoint") and the designer's go on its best fix. Principle 18. The designer's earlier note on this game: "Fun, but a bit too much."

**What a newcomer meets today:** tapping what looks suspicious loses in about 14 seconds to three strikes, and the label says only "False alarm". The list of what counts appears once, before play.

**The goal:** a newcomer's first shift teaches what counts by letting them be wrong safely, and tells them what each thing was.

**What done looks like:**
- **On day 1, the first two false alarms cost no strike.** The label names the item and says it's fine ("Phone: fine"), in place of "False alarm".
- **A missed item on day 1 is named too** ("Missed: knife"), where it went by.
- The strikes work as today from the third false alarm on, and on every later day.
- No new text before play.

**Out of bounds:** the arch, the scan, the item set and the scoring stay as they are (the look's second and third ideas are not in this step); no engine change.

**The designer will judge it** by the before-and-after GIF of a newcomer's first 20 seconds.
