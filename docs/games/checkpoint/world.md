# World: Checkpoint

## The drive

- **What you do:** scan bags and travellers and stop anything dangerous.
- **Why you do it:** protection.
- **For whom:** the passengers on today's flight.
- **How it ends:** day 10, the holiday rush: Pat's plane takes off, the radio says "Lane's yours", a new recruit gets your first-day words, then a departures board of your ten flights (`prd-v0.4.md`).

## Setting

A small regional airport's security lane, at dawn, ten working days. The belt is the X-ray screen (`style.md`).

## Places and things

- **The lane:** belt on the left, travellers on the right.
- **The flights:** one per day, listed in `prd-v0.3.md` B. Flight 10 is Pat's flight home.
- **Pat's desk:** her spare pen, glasses cord, knitted coaster, whistle, the LANE 3 sign and her mug, one per cleared day (`prd-v0.4.md` B).
- **The departures board:** the payoff; LANDED or GROUNDED per flight.

## Rules for this world

- The stakes are kind but real: a breach grounds the flight and passengers wait. No violence, no hijacking, no blame on a nationality or a look.
- Behaviour is flavour, never the tell; only the scans are.
