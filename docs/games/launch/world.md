# World: Launch

## The drive

- **What you do:** fling Mochi from the bakery's machine and fly him as far as he goes.
- **Why you do it:** love and longing.
- **For whom:** Daifuku, waiting 5 km away.
- **How it ends:** the reunion at Home, 5000 m, and then "keep going".

## Setting

A sweets countryside: the Bakery, Candy Meadow, Chocolate River, Soda Springs, Gingerbread Town, then Home among the strawberries. Soft pastel skies (`style.md`).
