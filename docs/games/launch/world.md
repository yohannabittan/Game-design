# World: Launch

## The drive

- **What you do:** fling Mochi from the bakery's machine and fly him as far as he goes.
- **Why you do it:** love and longing.
- **For whom:** Daifuku, waiting 5 km away.
- **How it ends:** a proposal at the tea-party window at 5000 m ("Will you be my sweetheart?" "Yes!"), then a honeymoon: they fly together.

## Setting

A sweets countryside: the Bakery, Candy Meadow, Chocolate River, Soda Springs, Gingerbread Town, then Home among the strawberries. Soft pastel skies (`style.md`).

## Places and things

- **The Bakery:** where Mochi starts, beside the Mochi Maker.
- **Candy Meadow, Chocolate River, Soda Springs, Gingerbread Town:** the stretches on the way.
- **Tea Party:** grandma's cottage at 5000 m, where Daifuku waits by the window (the place called Home before v0.6).
- **The gifts:** a sugar flower, a chocolate heart, a soda bubble shaped like a ring, a gingerbread ribbon.

## Rules for this world

- A cute love story that's a bit fun: sweet first, jokes from Mochi's nerves, no villains.

- Gentle: a crash is a squish and a dizzy face, never a hurt.
- Nothing needs knowledge of Japanese sweets; the names are just names.
