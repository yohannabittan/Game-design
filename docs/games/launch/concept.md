# Concept: Launch

- **Slug:** launch
- **Pattern:** P1 (aim and power) + P8 (run, earn, upgrade, run further)
- **Date:** 2026-09-30
- **Status:** concept, gate passed

## One sentence

Pull back a slingshot and let a round critter fly, tap to burn a little rocket fuel at the right moment, bounce off springs and birds to keep your speed, and go further than last time.

## The first ten seconds

A round orange critter sits in a slingshot on the left of a wide landscape screen, a sky fading right. You press anywhere and drag back; the band stretches and a dotted arc shows the first half-second of flight. You release: it flies, the camera follows, the ground scrolls under it, distance ticks up in the corner. It hits a spring pad and pops back up with a boing. It skids to a stop. A card: "212 m, best 212 m, +21 coins". Tap: back in the slingshot.

## The fantasy

You are the engineer of a very silly flight. Mastery is a launch so clean that every bounce lands on a spring and the fuel goes in exactly at the top of each arc.

## Three tips

1. Launch at about 40 degrees, not 45: the ground bounces pay better than the height.
2. Burn fuel on the way up, never on the way down; the same fuel buys more distance climbing.
3. Aim your landings: a spring hit at speed doubles your flight, a mud patch ends it.

## Perfect versus sloppy

A perfect run releases at the best angle, taps fuel at the rising half of each arc, and chains three springs: 600 m, a "Chain x3" call-out and a coin shower. A sloppy run launches high, burns fuel falling, lands in mud at 180 m and gets a flat thud.

## What it borrows

Learn to Fly and Burrito Bison: the pull of "one more run, I can afford the next upgrade", the camera chasing a tiny body over a huge distance, the bounces that feel like luck but are skill.

## Guessed kernel

Verb: drag to launch, tap to boost. Skill axis: launch angle and boost timing. Content: one endless seeded field with springs, birds and mud; a shop of four upgrades; five distance milestones. Session: one flight, 10 to 30 seconds; a sitting is several flights.

## Gate

- [x] The ten seconds are written and contain no text tutorial
- [x] Three tips are written and are about technique, not memorising layouts
- [x] Perfect and sloppy plays get visibly different responses
- [x] A naked run (base equipment, excellent skill) is possible by design: the first milestone at 500 m is reachable with no upgrades by a clean launch and two spring hits
- [ ] It works in portrait with one thumb: landscape, one thumb (ADR-0013: the flight is horizontal distance; portrait would show a sliver of the arc)
