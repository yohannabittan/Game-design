# Launch step: Tea Party you can't miss, together after, a caramel end worth having, and a new player's lessons (draft, 2026-11-21)

**From:** a first-time player's playtest (`../playtests/2026-11-21-first-time-player.md`) and the designer's "go for the Launch modifications".

## What the player gets

1. **Tea Party is a place you see coming.** Daifuku's home at the 5000 m line is a landmark readable from far off and at the flight's widest zoom (it can be about 30 px wide today): something that rises over the horizon before you reach it, so the arrival feels like an arrival.
2. **The scene plays when you get there.** Reaching her is the ending the run deserves, on the flight that reaches her, every time it is the first time on that save.
3. **Then they fly together.** After the proposal, Daifuku is with Mochi in every flight, visibly (riding along, holding on, flung with him), not only on the menu and the strip. The design promised "they continue the journey together".
4. **Caramel ends a flight in a way that's fun, not a dead stop.** Today a touch on caramel ends the flight on the spot. Options to be sketched; the designer picks.
5. **A new player gets the lessons on any phone.** A "New player" control that replays the three first-flight lessons (boost, glide, slam) without wiping the save; and the friends' release carries the lessons.

## Before building

A sketch (Sonnet) for the designer's eye: the landmark from far and near, the arrival beat, Daifuku riding along in a flight, and two or three caramel endings. The build follows the designer's picks. (5) is small and rides in the same build.
