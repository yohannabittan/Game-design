# Launch step: the first minute (locked 2026-11-14)

**From:** the first-minute look (`docs/first-minute/2026-11-14-launch-checkpoint.md`, "Launch") and the designer's go on its best fix. Principle 18.

**What a newcomer meets today:** a flight with no input at all lasts 18 seconds, says "New best!" and ticks off a goal. The boost is never needed, so a newcomer may never find it. The three verbs are told in one eight-word pill read mid-flight.

**The goal:** a newcomer uses the boost in their first flight, because the game had them do it, and meets the other two verbs one flight at a time.

**What done looks like:**
- **Flight one, on a fresh save:** at the top of the arc, a ghost thumb shows the hold for boost and the flight waits a beat for it (time slows; it never fails the player for missing it). Done once, it fades.
- **Flights two and three** teach glide, then slam, the same way, one each.
- The three-verb text pill goes on a fresh save; the ghost replaces it.
- **A flight with no input at all** doesn't say "New best!" on a fresh save's first flight: it says what to try ("Hold to boost").
- Each lesson shows once (save keys read with defaults).

**Out of bounds:** no change to the physics, the goals or the shop; no engine change.

**The designer will judge it** by the before-and-after GIF of a newcomer's first flight.
