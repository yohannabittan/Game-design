# Playtest: Launch v7 (first designer play)

- **Date:** 2026-09-30 (evening; from the designer's chat notes, expanded by the orchestrator)
- **Build:** cache launch-v7 (release v1.2 carries the same game)
- **Context:** phone, first play. Airplane mode: not stated.
- **Sessions this report covers:** 1. Best 700 m after buying two upgrades. Flights: about 3 or 4 early ones stuck at 400 to 450 m, then more.

## Delight test

- Did you want one more go after three minutes? Yes: "it was fun"; look, feel and art style "felt nice"; the critter "is cute like this".
- What made you stop: lack of content. Upgrades have no images and change nothing you can see (band, launcher, character); nothing new appears the further you go.

## Skill ladder check

- Discovered: the launch, the boost, that upgrades raise distance.
- Missed: boost as a way to steer onto a spring (tried, failed). Bird bounces (did not know what they do). That mud ends a flight.
- Harness gap: the careless model reaches 500 m on 198 of 200 flights with no upgrades; a real first-timer stalled at 400 to 450 m for 3 or 4 flights. The careless profile is too kind or the 500 m beat is harder to find than the model says.

## Content check

- Peak: the look, the critter, the first upgrades paying off (700 m).
- Trough: no visible change from upgrades, no new things with distance, no reason to reach a given distance.

## Wishes (the designer's words, condensed)

1. **Launch as a timing skill:** instead of pull and release, a needle oscillates through zones; the closer to 45 degrees when you stop it, the more boost. The skill of the launch becomes timing.
2. **Distance achievements that pay:** "reach X m, get Y coins", feeding unlockables, and unlockables should help you get further.
3. **More things the further you go.**
4. **Upgrades you can see:** images in the shop, and the band, launcher and character change when upgraded.
5. **A backstory for the critter** ("a yoga ball that decides to be an astronaut or something"). Once decided, the upgrades will make more sense.

## Decision input

- Evolve. Findings and proposed answers (PRD v0.2, pending the designer's calls on 1 and 5):
  - Launch verb: the designer's needle, replacing the drag. Needs a decision (changes the core verb and pattern P1).
  - Stars on the card: replace with distance goals that pay coins and show what is next (wish 2); milestones already pay a one-time bonus, but nothing says so.
  - Legibility: bird bounce shown on first contact; mud redrawn as a sticky pit that reads as danger; teaching springs made obviously bouncy, or cut.
  - Boost-to-spring: show where the current arc will land in flight so a boost can be aimed, or accept boost as distance only.
  - Content: zones by distance, each with its own look and objects (wish 3), themed by the backstory.
  - Visible upgrades: shop art and gear drawn on the critter and launcher (wish 4), themed by the backstory.
  - Backstory: needs a decision.
