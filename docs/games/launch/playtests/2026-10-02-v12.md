# Playtest: Launch v0.4 Build A (dev, launch-v12)

- **Date:** 2026-10-02 (from the designer's chat notes)
- **Build:** cache launch-v12

## Findings and wishes (the designer's words, condensed)

1. The mochi should become the parachute: he stretches his dough out, rather than putting on a sail.
2. The soda geyser should last longer.
3. A combo: hitting jellies in a row, or birds in a row, gives extra boost.
4. A speedometer, to know how fast he is going.
5. Cliffs sometimes send him the wrong way; the boost should reverse the direction.
6. More birds, at different heights.
7. The hot and cold drafts should reach higher; otherwise you can glide above everything.

## Decision input

- PRD v0.4 section I (Build A1): all seven, feel-first; Shop v2 (section H) follows.
