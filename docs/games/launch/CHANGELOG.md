# Launch changelog

## v0.1 (unreleased)
- Created from skeleton.
- Layer 1, mechanic (grey box), built by Opus. Pull from anywhere (angle and power from the drag, full pull at 140 px, under 14 px cancels) with a 0.6 s dotted arc; release launches. In flight a press fires a boost pulse along the flight direction; hold-to-boost exists behind Rocket 1, which is off in v0.1 (PRD open question: Rocket 1 only). Fixed 1/120 s physics with inputs stamped in flight time, so the same seed and inputs give the same flight at 30, 60 and 120 fps (`tools/sim-launch.mjs --fps`). Ground bounce and slide, springs (x1.25 vertical, at least 380, each used once per flight so a critter can never bounce on one spring forever), birds (upward speed at least 320, horizontal speed kept), mud (ends the flight), ramps (redirect along the slope); the chain multiplier x1, x1.5, x2, x3 on coins. The field is a fixed teaching chunk (spring at a clean full-pull launch's landing, a bird on the way down, the spring it carries you to, then mud) and endless seeded 300 m chunks drawn from a template table in four distance tiers; the fairness rules (a spring before every mud, no 150 m without a spring) are proved by `--check`. Camera keeps the critter at 30 percent of the width, no zoom; a height marker at the top edge when it is above the screen (PRD open question: no zoom). HUD: distance, chain, fuel pips, milestone call-out. End card: distance, best, coins with the best chain, milestone stars, seed, Launch Again, Menu. Coins, best and milestones persist (save v2; v1 was the demo). Upgrade levels live in TUNING at 0 (the shop is layer 4). Resolved open questions and tuning changes: airDrag 0.035 to 0.025, because at 0.035 total distance is capped near launch plus boost speed divided by drag and no expert with two upgrade levels could reach 5000 m; ramps keep all their speed (rampKeep 1, the plain reading of "redirects along its slope"); a new tier 3 from 3500 m is ramp-heavy; milestone bonuses 25, 50, 100, 200, 400 coins (the PRD gives none). Harness: `tools/sim-launch.mjs` (clean-launch proof and window, expert bot per upgrade set, naive bot, determinism, field check). Review: passed (release re-gate, v5; v6 fit fix checked by the orchestrator).

## v0.2 (unreleased)
- Build 1 (PRD v0.2 A, B, C, D, G; G2 and principle 11 for the card, menu and shop), built by Opus. **Needle launch:** the pull, dead zone, "Pull further", drag hint and dotted arc are gone; a needle sweeps a wedge at the launcher from 10 to 80 degrees and back in 1.6 s (triangle wave, starting at the bottom each flight), drawn in graded zones around sweetAngle: Perfect +-3, Great +-8, Good +-15, Weak elsewhere, launching at 1 / 0.9 / 0.8 / 0.55 of launchSpeedMax (Band applied), Perfect with 1 free pulse. A press anywhere stops it and launches at once (that press never boosts; Space does the same, then boosts); the zone's name pops above the launcher with its own chime and haptic, Perfect adds a gold flash and a spark ring. The angle is a pure function of the time since the launcher was ready, sampled at the tap. A fresh save shows a pulsing "Tap when the needle is in the gold" under the wedge until its first launch. **Goals instead of stars:** 18 goals in order (distance, skill, play), each a candy-kitchen name and a short condition, three active; a flight pays every active goal it meets as tickets on the card (at most three, then "+n"), with new places as "New place reached" tickets; the menu shows one number (best) and the three goals. Coins are now sugar. **Reading the world:** the first bird bounce of a save shows "Bird bounce: up and onward!" with an up arrow (under the bounce, never over the critter); mud is a dark pit sunk into the grass with bubbles, sheen and strands, with no raised pad or full-width rim; the first mud stop shows "Stuck! Jump mud with a boost" and holds the card back 2.4 s; springs bob at rest, carry a coil every 40 px, and show "Boing" with the height they give; a landing marker (the flight's own air step run forward, every 0.1 s and at once on a contact, hidden past the first contact, behind a bird or off the view) sits on the grass. **Steady**, the fifth upgrade, at 250 / 400 / 600: needle 12 percent slower and every zone 1 degree wider each side per level. **Teaching chunk:** now a run of springs laid out, by flying the real physics, for every Good-or-better stop at every Band, Aero and Steady level (spring A under every first landing, then springs of at most 55 m catching each next landing, to about 470 m, a bird on the Perfect stop's way down, the first mud at 510 m or later), so a Good launch reaches the first place and a Weak one falls short.
