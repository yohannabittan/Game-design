# Launch changelog

## v0.1 (unreleased)
- Created from skeleton.
- Layer 1, mechanic (grey box), built by Opus. Pull from anywhere (angle and power from the drag, full pull at 140 px, under 14 px cancels) with a 0.6 s dotted arc; release launches. In flight a press fires a boost pulse along the flight direction; hold-to-boost exists behind Rocket 1, which is off in v0.1 (PRD open question: Rocket 1 only). Fixed 1/120 s physics with inputs stamped in flight time, so the same seed and inputs give the same flight at 30, 60 and 120 fps (`tools/sim-launch.mjs --fps`). Ground bounce and slide, springs (x1.25 vertical, at least 380, each used once per flight so a critter can never bounce on one spring forever), birds (upward speed at least 320, horizontal speed kept), mud (ends the flight), ramps (redirect along the slope); the chain multiplier x1, x1.5, x2, x3 on coins. The field is a fixed teaching chunk (spring at a clean full-pull launch's landing, a bird on the way down, the spring it carries you to, then mud) and endless seeded 300 m chunks drawn from a template table in four distance tiers; the fairness rules (a spring before every mud, no 150 m without a spring) are proved by `--check`. Camera keeps the critter at 30 percent of the width, no zoom; a height marker at the top edge when it is above the screen (PRD open question: no zoom). HUD: distance, chain, fuel pips, milestone call-out. End card: distance, best, coins with the best chain, milestone stars, seed, Launch Again, Menu. Coins, best and milestones persist (save v2; v1 was the demo). Upgrade levels live in TUNING at 0 (the shop is layer 4). Resolved open questions and tuning changes: airDrag 0.035 to 0.025, because at 0.035 total distance is capped near launch plus boost speed divided by drag and no expert with two upgrade levels could reach 5000 m; ramps keep all their speed (rampKeep 1, the plain reading of "redirects along its slope"); a new tier 3 from 3500 m is ramp-heavy; milestone bonuses 25, 50, 100, 200, 400 coins (the PRD gives none). Harness: `tools/sim-launch.mjs` (clean-launch proof and window, expert bot per upgrade set, naive bot, determinism, field check). Review: pending.
- Layer 1 review round (launch-v2), built by Opus, per "Amendments after the layer 1 review". Camera zooms out with height, speed and the predicted landing, down to 0.45x, and slides the critter toward the left edge (to 10 percent of the width) when the landing is far; the ground band keeps its screen size and position, and when the critter is higher than 0.45x fits the sky pans with it, the band stays pinned at the bottom and a dotted drop line and "N m up" label show the height (replaces the off-screen height marker). Harness `--camera`, 200 expert seeds: off screen 0 percent of frames; landing on screen at the arc's top 83 percent at 844x390, 67 percent at 640x360 (90 percent needs a floor of 0.39 or 0.32); peak 29 px per frame. Fuel gauge moved top left, clear of the sling pocket (checked at 812x375 and 844x390 with 44 px insets). Mud has a light top rim (6.1:1 on sky, 4.2:1 on ground). The card ignores taps for 400 ms. Pull affordance: a ghost-thumb hint on a fresh save's first launch until the first pull, "Pull further" for a drag under the dead zone, the band turns white at full pull. Teaching chunk: first spring widened to 67 to 99 m (first landing from 0.84 power at 40 degrees) and the second to 150 to 214 m; the naked 500 m holds on all 200 seeds at 0.84 and 0.88 to 1 power, and 21 to 47.5 degrees at full pull. Upgrade model grouped and commented as the layer 4 shop's. The expert's p90 flight is 55 s (target under 60). Harness adds the review's sloppy human. Review: pending.
- Shop (launch-v3), built by Opus; prompts/04-progression.md rules, only the shop. A Shop button on the menu and the card (the card shows your coins on it and returns to itself); the shop shows coins and the four upgrades as cards with three level pips, the next level's effect in plain words, its price and Buy (greyed when short). Save v3 adds `up: { band, fuel, aero, rocket }` (levels 0 to 3); `migrate` from v2 adds it at all zero and keeps coins, best, milestones and flights (checked in Chromium from a seeded v2 save). Flights use the saved levels. Balance: Rocket 1 now matters alone: holding burns 1.5 fuel a second for 330 of thrust, twice a tap's push per fuel (expert median +238 m alone, +1100 m on top of Fuel 1); prices per level replace the PRD's 50, 150, 400: Band 300, 450, 650; Aero 300, 500, 700; Rocket 380, 550, 750; Fuel 1100, 1300, 1500 (Fuel is the strongest per level, so it is priced not to be the first buy). Harness `--buys` (30 seeds per state, all 256 states): best next buy per coin is Fuel in 99 states, Aero 96, Rocket 53, Band 7; the expert's greedy order from a fresh save starts Aero 1, Fuel 1, Rocket 1. `--pacing` (the sloppy human, cheapest level first): first purchase after flight 3, the last after flight 57. Each milestone is still reachable with at most two levels (5000 m: Fuel 2 on 151 of 200 seeds). The teaching chunk's middle spring runs to 232 m so a full pull with Aero 3 still lands on it. Review: pending.
- Juice and art (launch-v4), built by Opus; layers 3 and 5 together, per prompts/03-juice.md and 05-art.md. Style anchor in docs/games/launch/style.md: "flat round shapes, warm sky, soft shadows, one orange hero". Every PRD section 10 row: pull (band stretches, critter stretches along the pull, creak ticks rising with power, arc preview), launch (band twang, dust puff, 3 px camera kick, low hit, 12 ms), boost (flame puff, the spent fuel pip pops, whoosh, 6 ms), spring (pad compresses and pops, speed lines, "Boing", high hit, 10 ms), bird (feather burst, the bird tumbles away, two-note squawk, 8 ms), chain step (call-out grows, rising tone, coins pop: 3, 5, 8), mud (splat, the critter sticks flat, shake, miss, 30 ms), milestone (banner with a star, the distance pops, win, 20 ms), stop (the critter settles, the card slides up, buttons after the slide, coins count up, soft lose if no milestone). Art: the round orange critter with an ink outline and a light halo, eyes that track the flight, squash on landings; a sky from cream and rose by day through violet dusk (120 m) to indigo night with stars (300 m) at the top of the view, two parallax hill bands that thin out as the sky pans up; teal springs with coils, teal birds with flapping wings and yellow beaks, dark mud with a glossy rim, lilac plank ramps, a wooden fork with a rubber band that turns red-pink when taut; HUD on ink pills. All cosmetic: physics, field and camera code are unchanged (the arc preview matches the flight to 0 units over 1760 dots; the harness proofs are unchanged). Frame time at 4x CPU throttle on a high zoomed-out arc: median 1.1 ms, p95 3.3 ms. Also: "Hold to boost" shows for 3 s on the first flight after buying Rocket 1 (save v4 adds `holdTaught`, false after a migration); ledger calls for the flight result (distance, coins, bonus, chain, milestones, upgrade set), retry and quit (shop buys already logged); E.titleArea is the menu title for the release channel's TUNE taps.
