## v0.1 (unreleased)
- Created from skeleton.
- Layer 1, mechanic (grey box), built by Opus. Pull from anywhere (angle and power from the drag, full pull at 140 px, under 14 px cancels) with a 0.6 s dotted arc; release launches. In flight a press fires a boost pulse along the flight direction; hold-to-boost exists behind Rocket 1, which is off in v0.1 (PRD open question: Rocket 1 only). Fixed 1/120 s physics with inputs stamped in flight time, so the same seed and inputs give the same flight at 30, 60 and 120 fps (`tools/sim-launch.mjs --fps`). Ground bounce and slide, springs (x1.25 vertical, at least 380, each used once per flight so a critter can never bounce on one spring forever), birds (upward speed at least 320, horizontal speed kept), mud (ends the flight), ramps (redirect along the slope); the chain multiplier x1, x1.5, x2, x3 on coins. The field is a fixed teaching chunk (spring at a clean full-pull launch's landing, a bird on the way down, the spring it carries you to, then mud) and endless seeded 300 m chunks drawn from a template table in four distance tiers; the fairness rules (a spring before every mud, no 150 m without a spring) are proved by `--check`. Camera keeps the critter at 30 percent of the width, no zoom; a height marker at the top edge when it is above the screen (PRD open question: no zoom). HUD: distance, chain, fuel pips, milestone call-out. End card: distance, best, coins with the best chain, milestone stars, seed, Launch Again, Menu. Coins, best and milestones persist (save v2; v1 was the demo). Upgrade levels live in TUNING at 0 (the shop is layer 4). Resolved open questions and tuning changes: airDrag 0.035 to 0.025, because at 0.035 total distance is capped near launch plus boost speed divided by drag and no expert with two upgrade levels could reach 5000 m; ramps keep all their speed (rampKeep 1, the plain reading of "redirects along its slope"); a new tier 3 from 3500 m is ramp-heavy; milestone bonuses 25, 50, 100, 200, 400 coins (the PRD gives none). Harness: `tools/sim-launch.mjs` (clean-launch proof and window, expert bot per upgrade set, naive bot, determinism, field check).
