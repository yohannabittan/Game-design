# World: Gravity Golf

## The drive

- **What you do:** bend a small probe around planets, stars and black holes into a target, one gravity assist at a time.
- **Why you do it:** curiosity and discovery.
- **For whom:** humanity, waiting to hear whether we are alone.
- **How it ends (for now):** at the Galactic Core, a signal that is not natural. The next chapter is first contact.

## Setting

A probe's journey outward through six sectors, from a starfield near home to the galaxy's core. Deep blues and violets, glowing bodies (`style.md`).
