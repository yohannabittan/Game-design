# Concept: Gravity Golf

- **Slug:** gravity-golf
- **Pattern:** P1 Aim and launch (primary), L6 Boss cadence (in v0.1 as two boss holes), L7 Modes (later)
- **Date:** 2026-09-28
- **Status:** concept, gate passed

## One sentence

Drag to aim, release to launch, and sink the ball in as few shots as you can through walls, bumpers and gravity wells that bend its path.

## The first ten seconds

A dark field. A white ball near the bottom, a green-ringed hole near the top, one slate wall between them, offset to the left. The player touches anywhere and drags; a dotted line grows from the ball in the opposite direction of the drag, curving where it passes the wall's corner. They let go. The ball flies, taps the wall, slows, and drops into the hole with a burst of green. "1 shot. Par 2." Three stars pop in. A button says Next.

## The fantasy

Space mini golf. You are reading orbital physics by eye and threading a ball through it. Mastery looks like calling the shot before you release it.

## Three tips

1. Use less power than you think. The field is slick and the ball keeps rolling.
2. Bank off walls. They give back almost all the speed, and a wall shot is often straighter than a curve.
3. Wells pull harder up close. Aim to one side of a well and let it bend the ball around, not through.

## Perfect versus sloppy

Same hole: a well sits between ball and hole. The sloppy play aims straight, gets captured, and the ball spirals and stops behind the well: two more shots to recover. The perfect play aims wide with more power, the ball whips around the well's far side and rolls into the hole in one. The game gives the perfect play three stars, a bigger burst, the win chord, and a hole-in-one line on the card. The sloppy play gets one star and a quiet clear.

## What it borrows

The old Flash ball-to-hole game with bosses every few holes and gravity functions between them. What was good: every hole was a small physics puzzle you could solve in one shot if you read it right, and the boss holes tested everything the run had taught. Also gravity-golf games where the wells made trajectories feel alive.

## Guessed kernel

Verb: drag and release. Skill axis: angle and power against a visible physical field. Content: 10 authored holes, two of them boss holes. A session is one hole (10 to 60 seconds) or the run of 10 (about 5 minutes). Ends when the ball sinks.

## Gate

- [x] The ten seconds are written and contain no text tutorial
- [x] Three tips are written and are about technique, not memorising layouts
- [x] Perfect and sloppy plays get visibly different responses
- [x] A naked run (base equipment, excellent skill) is possible by design: there is no equipment in v0.1
- [x] It works in portrait with one thumb
