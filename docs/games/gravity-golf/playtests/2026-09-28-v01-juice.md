# Playtest: Gravity Golf v0.1 with juice (cache v4), look and feel notes

- **Date:** 2026-09-28
- **Context:** phone, alongside the v0.2 design discussion.

## Findings for the art layer

1. **The aim preview (range finder) is the right length but looks bad.** It is the element the player looks at on every shot, so it carries more feel than anything else. Decision: the art layer redesigns it. Candidates: evenly spaced dots that shrink and fade with distance; a comet-style tapering line; dots that inherit the ball's colour and glow near the launch. It must still be the honest simulation, and the shortened preview after hole 3 must still read as a deliberate cut, not a bug.
2. **The ball should look more fun.** A plain white disc reads as placeholder. Decision: a ball with character (a visible spin or a trailing glow tied to speed, a highlight, a faint shadow), still the largest-contrast object on the field.
3. **Planets and the space background need the space motif.** Decision: parallax starfield, nebula gradients, planets with bands, craters and rings, suns with a corona. This is the easy tier of the brand identity note and goes in the same art pass.

## Decision input

- These are art-layer items (layer 5) for v0.2, after the planet mechanic lands and passes review. None changes physics.
