# Playtest: Gravity Golf layer 1 (not yet v0.1)

- **Date:** 2026-09-28
- **Build:** commit 873e24c, cache gravity-golf-v1
- **Context:** phone, from the Pages URL. Layer 1 only: mechanic and hole 1, no juice, no content.
- **Sessions:** 1

## Delight test

- Wanted one more go: no.
- Why: "It's not fun because it's too easy to go out of bounds, too hard to land in the hole, it's more like curling than golf, and there are no gravity effects, which is needed for gravity golf."

## Findings and decisions

1. **Out of bounds is too easy.** Decision: the field is bordered by walls on all four sides in every hole. The ball bounces off the edges. Out of bounds is removed from v0.1 (PRD sections 3, 7, 10 and 16 amended).
2. **Curling, not golf.** The ball slides too far and the hole does not catch it. Decision: friction raised so the ball stops within about two seconds; the hole gets a capture pull near its rim so a slow ball that grazes it drops; sink speed raised; hole radius raised (PRD sections 7 and 16).
3. **No gravity effects.** Hole 1 has no well, and the well strength in the tuning table was too weak to bend a ball visibly at all. Decision: hole 1 gets a well beside the line so the first shot already curves and the preview shows it; well strength is set by an observable (a ball passing at 100 units and 400 units per second must deflect at least 25 degrees) that the builder verifies (PRD sections 9 and 16).
