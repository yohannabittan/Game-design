- **Expert:**
  - Holes 2, 3 and 4 took 1 or 2 tries each; they are tutorial holes, so that is expected.
  - Hole 1 took 9 tries, which is good, and hole 5 failed after 5, which is good.
  - The mode reads, and the rank-up from Expert stars fired (Blue Giant).
- **Rank and badges:** ranks 7 to 10 fired at 68, 70, 80 and 90 stars. Dark Matter and Great Attractor were found on 26 and 28. The secrets work as finds.
- **Bugs:** none seen. One telemetry detail: the export logs Expert as `blind=` and `from=blind`. This is internal, and only appears in the export.

## Decision input

- **Tighten 26, 28 and 29, after the weekly reset.**
  - 26 and 28: narrow the ace so it is a find, not the first try. Aim for a noisy-human rate of about 60 to 70 percent, against the shards' 97 to 100. Move the cup or a body so the aim window drops toward the 4° rule. The holes keep their lesson and their badge spots.
  - 29: narrow the release window to about 35 to 45 percent of the cycle, so the comet timing is the lesson again. Keep the two walls from the fix round.
  - Rough cost: one Sonnet shard of about 60 to 90 minutes plus the merge-gate checks and a short review, about $20 to 30 of API-equivalent (1 to 2 percent of the week).
- **Rule to add for future sectors:** a noisy-human ceiling as well as a floor. The last shot should be at least 60 percent and at most about 80 percent for normal holes after Sector 1, so late holes cannot be first-try gifts.
- Keep 27 and 30 as they are.
