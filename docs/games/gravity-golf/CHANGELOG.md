# Gravity Golf changelog

## v0.2 (unreleased)
- Mechanic delta (PRD v0.2 A, B, D, E) built by Opus 5.5, review pending: wells, repulsors, `wellStrength`, `wellMinDist`, `wellR` and `holdAccel` removed; planets are solid circles (wallBounce) that pull `planetGravity * mass / max(d, r)^2` from outside only, and a ball touching one below `landSpeed` lands and rests on it; a bumper is a planet with mass 0; suns bounce the ball and add `sunPenalty` per touch with `miss`, a 30 ms haptic, a stroke pop and a corona flare; movers on the hole clock: slide (as before), rotating bar (a capsule spinning at `barAngularSpeed`, collision uses its surface velocity so it pushes), orbiting moon (a small planet whose surface and pull move with it). Per-hole `stars { three, two }`, par is `two`, the card shows both thresholds. Level shape `name, ball, hole, stars, walls, planets, suns, movers`; holes 1 to 3 authored (First Light, Bank Shot, Slingshot), holes 4 to 10 are marked placeholders (walls and a cup, `three 1 / two 2`, each aceable) for the content shards. The preview still runs the real physics; trail and pull-ring juice now key off `planetNearR` (80, from the surface). Planets drawn with atmosphere, lit shading and a drifting pull ring; suns with a corona; moons with a faint orbit path; bars as slate capsules with a pivot dot.
- `planetGravity` stays at 4.5e6. The PRD D slingshot observable cannot hold as written: under friction 0.28 no value from 1e6 to 1.2e7 makes a 400 u/s pass at 20 units from a mass-1, radius-40 surface bend 90 degrees and leave (every pass bent that far falls back and lands); at 4.5e6 it bends 137 degrees and lands. The nearest form that holds at 4.5e6 is a pass at about 494 u/s: 107.5 degrees, still receding at 167 units. Measured with closest approach 60 from the centre, bend between the headings 200 path units before and after it. Escape rule holds with room: a full shot from a mass-1, radius-40 surface reaches 528 units, and every planet point on holes 1 to 3 reaches 355 or more.
- Open points resolved (simplest option): "20 units from its surface" is the ball centre 60 from the planet centre; the no-straight sweep's "no planet pass closer than 80" is measured from the planet or moon surface, as the orchestrator set for `tools/sim-golf.mjs`, which also prints the stricter from-the-centre count (hole 3 sinks nothing straight under either); with `holdAccel` gone, a slow ball in open field rests where the pull could not roll it faster than `stopSpeed` against friction (`stopSpeed * -ln(friction)`, 63.6 u/s squared, derived, not a tunable), so it never freezes on a bounce. Feel risk for the playtest: on holes 1 and 3 about 85% of random non-sinking shots end up landed on the planet, some after a 4 to 6 s roll back.
- Builder additions: `barW` 8 and `sunRearm` 9 in TUNING; bars pivot at their centre and spin clockwise, moons orbit clockwise, `phase` is radians at clock 0, slides take an optional `period`. A ball never comes to rest where a mover would sweep it (it waits and is knocked on); a ball that lands on a moon rides it and leaves with the moon's velocity added. A sun touch counts on entering contact and re-arms once the ball is `sunRearm` clear of the surface or is shot again.
- Save v4 stores stars per hole; the migration keeps stars won under the v0.1 pars and the v0.1 best strokes on the same hole numbers, which may be over-generous on the re-authored holes (accepted).
- `tools/sim-golf.mjs` (Opus 5.5) for the content shards: one shot, the no-straight sweep with aim and drag windows, the escape sweep with 1500 chained random shots, and three-star route checks (multi-shot, at 30/60/144 fps and jitter), all through `game.sim` on the real physics. Hole JSON format, commands and shard constraints are in `docs/games/gravity-golf/README.md`. game.js gains `game.sim` and `prepareLevel` only; behaviour unchanged.
- Tune panel `experiments`: planetGravity (1.5e6 to 1.35e7), landSpeed (20 to 150), friction (0.15 to 0.5), powerMax (500 to 1100), sunPenalty (0 to 3); physics reads them at step time, so a change applies on the next shot. All numbers above are at the defaults. `sw.js` CACHE_VERSION not bumped (layer may only touch `game.js`): bump at deploy.

## v0.1 (unreleased)
- Created from skeleton.
- Layer 1 (mechanic) built by Sonnet 5.5, passed review first time with four low-severity fixes (below): slingshot aim with occlusion rule, fixed 1/120 s physics shared by flight and preview, hole 1, strokes and par HUD, hole card with stars and best, menu with Play and mute; walls, bumpers, wells and mover are supported by the physics but only hole 1 is authored. Open questions resolved: preview simulates the real physics (wells and bounces included); the hole ring brightens while the ball is moving slower than sinkSpeed. Interpretation: out of bounds adds oobPenalty on top of the shot's own stroke.
- Layer 1 review fixes (Sonnet 5.5): hole 1 rebuilt so a near-straight shot at 30 to 70 px sinks over a 6.9 degree window (dead-straight now clears the wall; only aims more than about 3 degrees left clip it); `speedMax` clamp and `keyPowerStart` added to TUNING; `hit` plays at volume 0.3; pointercancel cancels the aim; Space no longer throws in the dead zone; saveVersion 2 with `migrate` dropping a non-object `best`.
- Layer 1 delta after the phone playtest (Sonnet 5.5, review pending): all four field edges are walls and out of bounds is gone; the cup pulls balls within captureR (44) at 900; holeR 18, sinkSpeed 380, friction 0.28, wellStrength 5.2e6; hole 1 rebuilt with a well beside the line (12 degree sink window). Two tuning changes beyond the PRD: stopSpeed 6 to 50, because with friction 0.28 a full-power shot only falls under 6 after 3.9 s and under 50 after 2.2 s (PRD wants about 2 s); and a new holdAccel 250 so a slow ball does not freeze at the top of its arc inside a well's pull. Well observables: with friction included the two PRD tests cannot both hold; strength 5.2e6 whips a 500 u/s pass at 40 units through 97 degrees without capture, while a 400 u/s pass at 100 units turns 13 degrees by closest approach and is then captured.
- Layer 2 content (Sonnet 5.5, review pending): holes 2 to 10 authored as data (bank, well on the line, corridor, boss well, repulsor, bumper field, two wells, needle gap, boss mover gate) with pars, boss flags, and a verified drag vector for each; per-well strength multipliers tune difficulty; hole select grid with locks and stars, total stars on the menu, sequential unlock, `win` on sink; saveVersion 3 adds `unlocked` and migrate rebuilds it from cleared holes.
- Review fixes for layers 1-2 (Sonnet 5.5, review pending): the pull inside wellMinDist now falls off linearly to zero so a well core is no longer a trap (escape sweep passes on every well); wellMinDist 28 to 44 and wellStrength 5.2e6 to 7e6 so the whip observable still holds (96 degrees at 40 units, 500 u/s), and every well multiplier is now 1.0 or less because a deeper well cannot be escaped at powerMax under friction 0.28 (so no boss well is bigger than a normal one); holes 3, 5, 8, 9 re-laid out and all ten solutions re-verified; the edge walls are fitted inside the screen at any aspect ratio (view() reserves borderW); hole 4 rebuilt under the ball with an 11 degree window; a Retry button in the HUD, live only while the ball is at rest; a ball pressed against a wall by a well now rests instead of timing out; per-frame allocations hoisted.
- Layer 3 juice (Sonnet 5.5, review pending): every row of the PRD juice table plus drag glow, release flash and puff, spark bursts with a big-hit tier (`juice.bigHitSpeed`, shake only above it), well trail and faster rings near the ball, boss banner and boss well colour, sink burst (bigger on three stars, `coin` then `win`), card slide with staggered star pops and buttons held back until after the beat, strokes number pop, retry fade, far-rest pulse, menu tile pop; all effect numbers in `TUNING.juice`, particles capped by `particleCap`, physics and level data byte-identical. Engine gaps worked around, for a possible skeleton ADR: no delayed `E.audio.play` (a zero-motion tween stands in), `E.button` and `E.text` reset `globalAlpha` (buttons pop by scale instead of fading), particles are screen-space only (mapped through `view()`). `sw.js` CACHE_VERSION not bumped (layer may only touch `game.js`): bump at deploy.
