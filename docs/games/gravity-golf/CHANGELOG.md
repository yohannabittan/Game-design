# Gravity Golf changelog

## v0.3 (unreleased)
- Mechanic delta (PRD v0.3 A, D, D2, E) built by Opus 5.5; the v8 review passed it for deploy with four fixes (C3, below). **Comets**: mover type `comet` (`a`, `b`, `period`, optional `r` defaulting to `cometR` 10) crossing from `a` to `b` at constant speed, then back at `a` when the clock wraps; no pull; on contact the ball gets a `wallBounce` reflection off the comet's surface plus `cometPush` (0.8) times the comet's velocity, once per contact. Its path counts as a mover sweep for the rest rule. Drawn as a cyan head with a near-white core, a glow and a tail along its motion that grows from nothing at `a`, over a faint dashed path; a kick adds a cyan spark burst, a short shake and a buzz. **Black holes**: level list `blackholes: [{ x, y, r, mass }]` (`r` defaults to `holeR2` 26, `mass` to `bhMass` 1.4); pull `planetGravity * mass / max(d, bhPullR)^2`; a ball whose centre crosses the horizon is swallowed: `bhPenalty` (1) stroke pops on the HUD with `miss`, a falling tone and a 45 ms buzz, the horizon flares, the ball spirals in over 0.7 s and fades back in at the last rest (the point the shot started from; a moon rider goes back onto its moon at clock 0). The preview stops at the horizon. Drawn as a black core, a thin pale-violet horizon ring, a soft purple glow, three clockwise swirl arms and rings drifting in to the horizon. Section E names in TUNING; physics reads every value at step time. The trail turns purple within `planetNearR` of a horizon as it does near planets. Menu badges show a black hole, else a comet, before the older kinds. Existing ten holes: outcomes byte-identical to v0.2 before the D2 layout fixes (the harness table was diffed).
- Decided where the PRD was open (simplest option): the comet kick is once per contact and re-arms when the ball is `cometRearm` (9, builder addition, like `sunRearm`) clear or is shot again; if the comet is still catching the ball after the kick (a slow ball it overtakes) it pushes it on like a moving wall (the ball's velocity relative to the comet is reflected with `wallBounce`), so it cannot pass through the ball or kick it twice. The kick counts as a bounce (sound, sparks, and a bounce for the no-straight sweep). A comet ignores walls and planets (shards keep its path clear). A comet's `a` and `b` may lie outside the field. Black holes count as pulling bodies in the sweep's "no pass within 80" test, measured from the horizon.
- PRD v0.3 D gravity experiment: tune presets Heavy (4.5e6, 50, the defaults the routes are proven at), Medium (3e6, 50), Light (2e6, 50) and "Light+roll" (3e6, 80): the PRD's "Light and rolling" measures 130 px at the panel's 14 px and the engine's four-up preset buttons are 81.5 px wide at 390 px, so the label is shortened; values as the PRD. New `stopSpeed` slider (20 to 120); all six sliders kept (planetGravity, stopSpeed, landSpeed, friction, powerMax, sunPenalty; the last ends 526 px down a 844 px screen). Presets apply live and persist across a reload (checked in Chromium, and Reset restores 4.5e6 / 50).

## v0.2 (unreleased)
- Mechanic delta (PRD v0.2 A, B, D, E) built by Opus 5.5, review pending: wells, repulsors, `wellStrength`, `wellMinDist`, `wellR` and `holdAccel` removed; planets are solid circles (wallBounce) that pull `planetGravity * mass / max(d, r)^2` from outside only, and a ball touching one below `landSpeed` lands and rests on it; a bumper is a planet with mass 0; suns bounce the ball and add `sunPenalty` per touch with `miss`, a 30 ms haptic, a stroke pop and a corona flare; movers on the hole clock: slide (as before), rotating bar (a capsule spinning at `barAngularSpeed`, collision uses its surface velocity so it pushes), orbiting moon (a small planet whose surface and pull move with it). Per-hole `stars { three, two }`, par is `two`, the card shows both thresholds. Level shape `name, ball, hole, stars, walls, planets, suns, movers`; holes 1 to 3 authored (First Light, Bank Shot, Slingshot); holes 4 to 10 were placeholders at this step and were then authored by the content shards (the last two lines of this section). The preview still runs the real physics; trail and pull-ring juice now key off `planetNearR` (80, from the surface). Planets drawn with atmosphere, lit shading and a drifting pull ring; suns with a corona; moons with a faint orbit path; bars as slate capsules with a pivot dot.
- `planetGravity` stays at 4.5e6. The PRD D slingshot observable cannot hold as written: under friction 0.28 no value from 1e6 to 1.2e7 makes a 400 u/s pass at 20 units from a mass-1, radius-40 surface bend 90 degrees and leave (every pass bent that far falls back and lands); at 4.5e6 it bends 137 degrees and lands. The nearest form that holds at 4.5e6 is a pass at about 494 u/s: 107.5 degrees, still receding at 167 units. Measured with closest approach 60 from the centre, bend between the headings 200 path units before and after it. Escape rule holds with room: a full shot from a mass-1, radius-40 surface reaches 528 units, and every planet point on holes 1 to 3 reaches 355 or more.
- Open points resolved (simplest option): "20 units from its surface" is the ball centre 60 from the planet centre; the no-straight sweep's "no planet pass closer than 80" is measured from the planet or moon surface, as the orchestrator set for `tools/sim-golf.mjs`, which also prints the stricter from-the-centre count (hole 3 sinks nothing straight under either); with `holdAccel` gone, a slow ball in open field rests where the pull could not roll it faster than `stopSpeed` against friction (`stopSpeed * -ln(friction)`, 63.6 u/s squared, derived, not a tunable), so it never freezes on a bounce. Feel risk for the playtest: on holes 1 and 3 about 85% of random non-sinking shots end up landed on the planet, some after a 4 to 6 s roll back.
- Builder additions: `barW` 8 and `sunRearm` 9 in TUNING; bars pivot at their centre and spin clockwise, moons orbit clockwise, `phase` is radians at clock 0, slides take an optional `period`. A ball never comes to rest where a mover would sweep it (it waits and is knocked on); a ball that lands on a moon rides it and leaves with the moon's velocity added. A sun touch counts on entering contact and re-arms once the ball is `sunRearm` clear of the surface or is shot again.
- Save v4 stores stars per hole; the migration keeps stars won under the v0.1 pars and the v0.1 best strokes on the same hole numbers, which may be over-generous on the re-authored holes (accepted).
- `tools/sim-golf.mjs` (Opus 5.5) for the content shards: one shot, the no-straight sweep with aim and drag windows, the escape sweep with 1500 chained random shots, and three-star route checks (multi-shot, at 30/60/144 fps and jitter), all through `game.sim` on the real physics. Hole JSON format, commands and shard constraints are in `docs/games/gravity-golf/README.md`. game.js gains `game.sim` and `prepareLevel` only; behaviour unchanged.
- Tune panel `experiments`: planetGravity (1.5e6 to 1.35e7), landSpeed (20 to 150), friction (0.15 to 0.5), powerMax (500 to 1100), sunPenalty (0 to 3); physics reads them at step time, so a change applies on the next shot. All numbers above are at the defaults. `sw.js` CACHE_VERSION was bumped at deploy (v0.2 complete shipped as `gravity-golf-v7`).
- Layer 5 art (Sonnet 5.5, review pending), `game.js` only, no assets: every colour in `TUNING.art.palette` (old top-level and juice colour keys removed; slate lightened to #64748b for 3.7:1 on the field), three text sizes (15, 20, 36; heavy weight on 36 only) and HUD as label-over-value stacks with the hole name; seeded two-layer parallax starfield (Path2D, drifts up to 2.5 and 6 units with the ball) and one or two nebulae per hole; planets with a look from position and mass (craters below 0.75, bands, ring system above 1.2), atmosphere ring, shaded limb and crisp edge, bumpers as matte slate spheres, moons pale with a crater, suns with a core, two counter-rotating soft ray sets and a breathing shimmer, walls, slides (with a rail) and bars as one bevelled slate metal with bolts and a pivot; cup with halo, glow that eases toward sinkable and a flag; ball with a soft shadow to the lower right, highlight, dark rim and a spin seam that turns with the roll; trail now shows in open flight as well (pale in space, purple near planets) with strength tied to speed; range finder redesigned (dots shrink and fade over the preview's own length, white toward orange with power, brighter first segment, power gauge ring on the ball, soft end cap only when the preview was cut by time); menu tiles are small planets, suns, bumpers or slabs from each hole's main feature (boss tiles magenta), sky covers the whole screen on the menu and cards. Effect numbers changed outside `art`: `juice.corona` 1.7 to 1.5, `juice.coronaAlpha` 0.45 to 0.7 (the rays carry the sun's reach now). Physics, LEVELS, scoring, timing and saves untouched (diffed against HEAD). Frame cost on hole 8 at 4x CPU throttle in headless Chromium: update plus render JS about 1 ms mean, under 6 ms worst; with a forced software raster flush about 8 to 14 ms against 5 to 7 ms before the art, the extra being gradient fills that a phone GPU does not pay.

## v0.1 (unreleased)
- Created from skeleton.
- Layer 1 (mechanic) built by Sonnet 5.5, passed review first time with four low-severity fixes (below): slingshot aim with occlusion rule, fixed 1/120 s physics shared by flight and preview, hole 1, strokes and par HUD, hole card with stars and best, menu with Play and mute; walls, bumpers, wells and mover are supported by the physics but only hole 1 is authored. Open questions resolved: preview simulates the real physics (wells and bounces included); the hole ring brightens while the ball is moving slower than sinkSpeed. Interpretation: out of bounds adds oobPenalty on top of the shot's own stroke.
- Layer 1 review fixes (Sonnet 5.5): hole 1 rebuilt so a near-straight shot at 30 to 70 px sinks over a 6.9 degree window (dead-straight now clears the wall; only aims more than about 3 degrees left clip it); `speedMax` clamp and `keyPowerStart` added to TUNING; `hit` plays at volume 0.3; pointercancel cancels the aim; Space no longer throws in the dead zone; saveVersion 2 with `migrate` dropping a non-object `best`.
- Layer 1 delta after the phone playtest (Sonnet 5.5, review pending): all four field edges are walls and out of bounds is gone; the cup pulls balls within captureR (44) at 900; holeR 18, sinkSpeed 380, friction 0.28, wellStrength 5.2e6; hole 1 rebuilt with a well beside the line (12 degree sink window). Two tuning changes beyond the PRD: stopSpeed 6 to 50, because with friction 0.28 a full-power shot only falls under 6 after 3.9 s and under 50 after 2.2 s (PRD wants about 2 s); and a new holdAccel 250 so a slow ball does not freeze at the top of its arc inside a well's pull. Well observables: with friction included the two PRD tests cannot both hold; strength 5.2e6 whips a 500 u/s pass at 40 units through 97 degrees without capture, while a 400 u/s pass at 100 units turns 13 degrees by closest approach and is then captured.
- Layer 2 content (Sonnet 5.5, review pending): holes 2 to 10 authored as data (bank, well on the line, corridor, boss well, repulsor, bumper field, two wells, needle gap, boss mover gate) with pars, boss flags, and a verified drag vector for each; per-well strength multipliers tune difficulty; hole select grid with locks and stars, total stars on the menu, sequential unlock, `win` on sink; saveVersion 3 adds `unlocked` and migrate rebuilds it from cleared holes.
- Review fixes for layers 1-2 (Sonnet 5.5, review pending): the pull inside wellMinDist now falls off linearly to zero so a well core is no longer a trap (escape sweep passes on every well); wellMinDist 28 to 44 and wellStrength 5.2e6 to 7e6 so the whip observable still holds (96 degrees at 40 units, 500 u/s), and every well multiplier is now 1.0 or less because a deeper well cannot be escaped at powerMax under friction 0.28 (so no boss well is bigger than a normal one); holes 3, 5, 8, 9 re-laid out and all ten solutions re-verified; the edge walls are fitted inside the screen at any aspect ratio (view() reserves borderW); hole 4 rebuilt under the ball with an 11 degree window; a Retry button in the HUD, live only while the ball is at rest; a ball pressed against a wall by a well now rests instead of timing out; per-frame allocations hoisted.
- Layer 3 juice (Sonnet 5.5, review pending): every row of the PRD juice table plus drag glow, release flash and puff, spark bursts with a big-hit tier (`juice.bigHitSpeed`, shake only above it), well trail and faster rings near the ball, boss banner and boss well colour, sink burst (bigger on three stars, `coin` then `win`), card slide with staggered star pops and buttons held back until after the beat, strokes number pop, retry fade, far-rest pulse, menu tile pop; all effect numbers in `TUNING.juice`, particles capped by `particleCap`, physics and level data byte-identical. Engine gaps worked around, for a possible skeleton ADR: no delayed `E.audio.play` (a zero-motion tween stands in), `E.button` and `E.text` reset `globalAlpha` (buttons pop by scale instead of fading), particles are screen-space only (mapped through `view()`). `sw.js` CACHE_VERSION was bumped at deploy (`gravity-golf-v4`, the juice playtest build).
