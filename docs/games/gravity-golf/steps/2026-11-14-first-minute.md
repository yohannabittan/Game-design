# Gravity Golf step: the first minute (locked 2026-11-14)

**From:** the first-minute look (`docs/first-minute/2026-11-14-golf-ink-recoil.md`, "Gravity Golf") and the designer's go on its best fix. Principles 18 and 21.

**What a newcomer meets today:** four taps and about 6 seconds of story before the first drag, and the story teaches nothing about the drag. On the result card, the star target ("3 stars: 1 shot") is the smallest text and Next is the loudest button.

**The goal:** a newcomer's first touch is a shot, and after a hole they can see at a glance what a better result needed.

**What done looks like:**
- **On a fresh save, Play goes straight to hole 1.** The cold-open story plays after hole 1's result card (once), and stays on the menu's Story button.
- **Hole 1's hint** is at most eight words and says which way the ball goes ("Drag back. Release. Dots show the shot.").
- **On a 1 or 2 star result,** the star target is the card's headline ("3 stars: 1 shot"), and on holes 1 to 5 Retry is the main button with Next second. On 3 stars, Next is the main button as today.
- **Check the one thing the look couldn't verify:** a scripted upward drag sent the ball down while the preview pointed up. Find out whether a real drag can do this; if it can, say exactly when, and fix it only if the fix is small and certain.

**Out of bounds:** no change to holes, physics, par or missions; no engine change.

**The designer will judge it** by the before-and-after GIF of a newcomer's first 20 seconds.
