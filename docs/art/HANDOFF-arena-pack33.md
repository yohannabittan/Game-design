## Why these picks
- **Style:** the three options differ in finish. A is flat bold shapes with a thick dark outline (sticker-like), B painted with soft cel shading, C richer with rim light and engraved detail. For the objects I picked A: it keeps its silhouette and colour at 64 px, and sits closest to the gear icons. The masks in B (two masks) and C (finer detail) lose the "half smiling, half sly" read at 64 px; fourth's A shows four distinct tablets with rings.
- **Fighter:** B has the clearest face and the tallest crest at 64 px. C is more detailed but its red hair merges with the cloak. The rest were painted to match the picks (objects from `unlock-trick-A`, busts from `fighter-sparta-B`).
- If you pick differently, I redo the dependent images (about US$0.70 for the objects, US$0.12 for the busts).

## Known weaknesses (for wiring)
