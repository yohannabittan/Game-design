# Starting an X account from zero: what the guides say (2026-11-20)

Read by web search only (x.com is unreachable from the container). Almost all sources are vendor blogs and creator posts: treat every number as unverified and directional. Sources at the end.

## What most sources agree on

1. **Replies are the lever for a new account.** Commonly quoted: about 80% replies under bigger accounts' posts, 20% your own posts, daily, for around ten weeks before growth shows. A useful reply under a post with many views puts your profile in front of that audience.
2. **One clear niche.** Accounts about everything grow slowly.
3. **The hook (first line) decides.** Timing and hook strength are cited as the top predictors of reach.
4. **No links in the main post.** Off-platform links are reported to lose most of their reach (50–90%, and near zero for non-Premium accounts per one 2026 guide). Put the link in the first reply.
5. **Video wins.** Short clips of gameplay or funny moments beat stills. Some guides rank TikTok and YouTube Shorts above X for virality, with X as the place for community and developer networking.
6. **Show the process, not the polish.** Audiences skip trailers but watch a developer fight a bug and explain a design choice.
7. **Premium helps reach** (estimates of 2x to 10x), and reply ranking may favour Premium accounts.
8. **Regular beats frequent.** Cadence advice conflicts (3–7 a week, or 1–3 a day). Never post in bursts: 5–6 posts in an hour is reported to trip spam filters.

## What it means for us

- **Replies need a human.** The biggest lever is conversation, and automated replies are spam (and against X's rules). That is a daily habit for the designer, which pulls against "minimal input" and "never a duty". Options: keep it to something like 10 minutes a day when it's fun, or rely more on the things that spread on their own (newsgames, clips) and accept slower growth.
- **The factory can carry the rest:** drafting posts, cutting GIFs and clips from builds (`tools/film.mjs`, `tools/sketch.mjs` already make them), newsgames, and threads of learnings. The designer approves each post; nothing goes out without a yes.
- **The niche:** "agents that build games", one sentence. Games for the clips, factory for the substance.
- **Links to games go in the first reply**, with the clip in the main post.
- **Cross-post the clips** to TikTok and Shorts, where virality is reportedly higher.
- **Premium** is a small monthly cost that the sources say matters. The designer decides.
