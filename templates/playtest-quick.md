# Quick playtest capture (from the phone)

Send these five lines in chat right after playing. The orchestrator expands them into `templates/playtest-report.md` under `docs/games/<slug>/playtests/` the same hour and maps each finding to a PRD line.

1. Game and version, minutes played, offline yes/no.
2. Best moment (the level, stencil or challenge, and what happened).
3. Worst moment, and where you got stuck (how many tries).
4. One wish.
5. TUNE preset you settled on, if you touched it.

Optional: any number you noticed (strokes, stars, percent, score).
