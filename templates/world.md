# World: <Game title>

One page, written before any story, voice line or character art (`docs/games/<slug>/world.md`). Story cards, result-card voices, flavour lines and portrait briefs all come from here, so a name or a personality never drifts between builds. Every name is original: no real person, film, studio or product (the parody rule).

## The drive

- **What you do:** the action, in one line.
- **Why you do it:** the emotional drive, such as love, ambition, care, protection or curiosity.
- **For whom:** the person or people it is for.
- **How it ends:** the payoff that closes the drive.
