# Synthesis: GAME_TITLE

Half a page for the designer to react to before any concept card or PRD is written. Plain language, no implementation. The reaction shapes the concept card.

**In one line.** What you do and what ends a session.

**Why it is fun.** The tension or feeling the mechanic produces. What the player feels themselves getting better at within one session. Session length and why it fits the phone.

**The mastery ladder.** Intermediate tips (1 to 3). Expert tips (3 to 5). What legendary looks like.

**The one hard problem.** The thing most likely to make it not fun, and which layer will find out.

**Minimum lovable version.** Content count, what ends a session, what the reward is.

**Where it grows.** Progression hooks, smart randomness, modes.

**What I need your reaction on.** Two to four questions whose answers change the design: theme, primary pressure, the reward object, which alternative to start instead.
